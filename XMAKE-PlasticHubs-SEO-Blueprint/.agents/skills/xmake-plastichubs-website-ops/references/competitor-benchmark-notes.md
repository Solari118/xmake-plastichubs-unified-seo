# Competitor Benchmark Notes

Use competitor benchmarks only when the user provides them or asks for a benchmark pass. Do not invent competitor claims or imply external research was performed when it was not.

## What To Extract

Extract patterns, not copy:

- page structure
- buyer-intent sections
- useful comparison tables
- quote or RFQ flow
- FAQ coverage
- internal link patterns
- practical specification tables
- claim-control wording

## What Not To Borrow

Do not copy:

- proprietary wording
- unsupported claims
- brand positioning that conflicts with Xmake or Plastichubs
- broad marketplace framing for Plastichubs
- certification or equipment claims without source evidence

## Evidence Label

When a recommendation comes from benchmarks, label it as `benchmark-based`.
