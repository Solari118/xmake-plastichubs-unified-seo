# Brand And Evidence Policy

## Public Brand

- Every artifact selects exactly one public brand: XMAKE or PlasticHubs.
- Never mention Plastichubs/xmake in public body copy, metadata, CTA, headings, image text, schema, or publishable source notes.
- Shared-supply-chain data can be expressed as the selected brand's capability without public attribution to Plastichubs/xmake.

## Applicability

- Confirm that shared data applies to the selected brand, process, material, facility context, and service scope.
- PlasticHubs is plastic-manufacturing-first. Do not assign it metal CNC, die casting, or broad sheet-metal services.
- XMAKE may use its broader supported manufacturing scope.
- Ordinary XMAKE manufacturing, finishing, self-operated factory, owned-equipment, engineering, inspection, and supply-chain capability are project-approved defaults. Do not require independently searchable Chinese factory or supplier records for those general statements.
- Continue to verify exact figures, named equipment, certifications, statutory qualifications, regulated work, guarantees, safety claims, and measurable acceptance limits.

## Evidence Levels

Prefer, in order:

1. user-provided project data and approved internal knowledge
2. current official company information
3. official standards and primary technical sources
4. established engineering references
5. clearly labeled professional judgment

Exact metrics, standards, certifications, guarantees, tolerances, lead times, prices, and competitor claims require evidence. Ordinary technical variability should use precise qualifiers rather than false certainty.

## Public Outbound Links And Attribution

- Public copy defaults to same-brand, same-domain internal links.
- Third-party names, URLs, citations, `Source:` labels, competitor links, and public research attribution are prohibited unless explicitly approved.
- External research stays in a private ledger marked `Internal — Not for Publication`.
- Citation readiness means internal traceability, not public outbound linking.
- If safe publication requires external attribution, use `Needs approval`, remove the claim, or replace it with approved brand-owned evidence.
- Compensated outbound placements require commercial approval and `rel="sponsored"` or `rel="nofollow"`.
