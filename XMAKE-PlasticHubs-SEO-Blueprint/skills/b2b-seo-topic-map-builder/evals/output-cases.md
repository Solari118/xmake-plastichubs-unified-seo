# Output Evaluation Cases

Use these cases for regression review.

1. Informational plastic material guide: expect PlasticHubs, `soft`, natural keyword variants, no forced broker section.
2. CNC supplier comparison: expect XMAKE or PlasticHubs by supplied scope, `explicit`, sourcing-accountability criteria, and evidence requirements.
3. ISO standard explainer: expect `none` or narrowly justified `soft`, exact standard verification, and no invented certification scope.
4. Injection-molding troubleshooting: expect failure-analysis structure, conditional heuristics, optional FAQ, and no fixed article template.
5. Topic-map request without live keyword data: expect strategic candidates, no volume/CPC claims, and `SERP Check Needed` markers.
6. Final article brief with locale-capable organic results: expect five URLs, environment metadata, `Validated`, per-page analysis, ranking hypotheses, and differentiation requirements.
7. Search tool without reliable locale or exact positions: expect `Best-effort`, transparent limitations, and no false exact-ranking claim.
8. CNC-versus-3D-printing comparison: expect intent completion criteria, a shared-criteria decision module, and no separate “when to choose” sections that repeat the same matrix.
9. Global-audience article with a user-set U.S. grade 9-12 target and 2,000-word minimum: expect both constraints preserved as task-specific fields, not global defaults.
10. Bilingual review followed by WordPress-ready Word delivery: expect the review stage, deliverable mode, preview format, and Word style reference to remain explicit across handoff.

For every case, assert that all contract fields exist and Plastichubs/xmake is absent from public variables.
