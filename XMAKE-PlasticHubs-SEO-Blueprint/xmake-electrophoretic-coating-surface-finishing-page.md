## H2：什么是电泳涂装 / What Is Electrophoretic Coating

Electrophoretic coating 也称 E-coating 或 electrodeposition coating。工艺中，经过清洗和前处理的导电零件浸入水性涂料槽。电场使带电涂料颗粒向零件表面迁移并沉积，随后通过清洗和固化形成涂层。

它与普通喷涂的主要区别在于沉积方式。浸没和电沉积有助于覆盖许多边缘、凹槽、孔位周围和内腔区域，因此适合需要较一致覆盖的复杂金属零件。但零件结构仍会影响电场分布、排液、挂具接触和局部膜厚，不能把“均匀”理解为所有位置完全相同。

Electrophoretic coating is also called E-coating or electrodeposition coating. Cleaned and pretreated conductive parts are immersed in a water-based coating bath. An electric field moves charged coating particles toward the part surface, where they deposit. The coated part is then rinsed and cured to form a continuous film.

The main difference from conventional spray coating is the deposition method. Immersion and electrodeposition help reach many edges, grooves, areas around holes, and internal cavities, making the process useful for complex metal parts that need more consistent coverage. Part geometry still affects electric-field distribution, drainage, rack contact, and local film thickness, so “uniform” should not be read as identical thickness at every location.

Cathodic acrylic E-coating is more relevant when metal parts need a balance of appearance, color, and protection. Suitability as a final visible layer depends on the specific chemistry, color control, service environment, and acceptance requirements.

Anodic E-coating can be used for selected metal coating projects, but its applications and performance priorities differ from cathodic systems. Surface Effect: Continuous film · Uniform appearance · Project-dependent

## H2：适用金属与零件条件 / Compatible Metals and Part Conditions

### 钢 / Steel

常见流程包括清洗、除油、前处理、电沉积、清洗和固化。钢材状态、焊缝、氧化物、锐边和封闭间隙会影响前处理与覆盖结果。

A typical route includes cleaning, degreasing, pretreatment, electrodeposition, rinsing, and curing. Steel condition, welds, oxides, sharp edges, and enclosed gaps affect pretreatment and coverage.

### 铝 / Aluminum

铝件需要与合金、表面状态和涂层体系相匹配的前处理，并确认导电接触、挂具和固化条件。不能把钢件的前处理直接套用于铝件。

Aluminum requires pretreatment matched to the alloy, surface condition, and coating system, plus review of electrical contact, racking, and curing conditions. A steel pretreatment route should not be applied to aluminum by default.

### 镀锌钢 / Galvanized Steel

需要评估锌层状态、清洗和前处理兼容性、附着力以及固化条件。目标防腐性能应按完整体系确认，而不是把镀锌层与 E-coating 的作用简单相加。

Zinc-layer condition, cleaning and pretreatment compatibility, adhesion, and curing conditions require review. Target corrosion performance should be confirmed for the complete system rather than assumed by simply combining galvanizing and E-coating.

### 带内腔、凹槽或装配结构的金属零件 / Metal Parts with Cavities, Recesses, or Assemblies

电沉积通常比仅靠喷涂更容易覆盖部分内腔和凹陷，但必须检查排液、排气、挂具方向、封闭接缝和局部膜厚。完全封闭或难以排液的结构仍可能不适合直接处理。

Electrodeposition can reach some cavities and recessed areas more effectively than spray-only application, but drainage, venting, rack orientation, enclosed joints, and local film thickness must be reviewed. Fully enclosed or poorly drained geometry may still be unsuitable without design or process changes.

## H2：电泳涂装的优势与限制 / E-coating Advantages and Limitations

### 优势 / Advantages

- 复杂几何覆盖：浸没和电沉积有助于涂层进入许多边缘、凹槽、孔位周围和内腔区域。
- 防腐基础：可作为钢、铝及其他经评估金属的保护性涂层或涂层体系底层。
- 膜层连续：适合需要较一致覆盖而单纯喷涂难以触达的零件结构。
- 后续涂层兼容规划：可与喷粉或喷漆组成多层涂层体系。

- Complex-geometry coverage: Immersion and electrodeposition help coating reach many edges, grooves, areas around holes, and cavities.
- Corrosion-protective base: Can serve as a protective coat or coating-system base for steel, aluminum, and other reviewed metals.
- Continuous film: Useful for structures where spray-only application has difficulty reaching all required areas consistently.
- Downstream coating planning: Can be combined with powder coating or painting in a multilayer coating stack.

### 限制与设计注意事项 / Limitations and Design Notes

- 工艺依赖导电基材，不应作为普通塑料表面处理描述。
- 膜厚会影响螺纹、孔、压配合、密封面和导电接触面，必要时需要遮蔽或后加工。
- 盲孔、封闭内腔、搭接缝和复杂装配可能导致排液、排气或涂层堆积问题。
- 挂点必须保持导电接触，可能留下局部接触痕迹，位置需要提前定义。
- 防腐、颜色、外观和耐候性均取决于具体前处理、涂料体系、固化和使用环境。

- The process depends on a conductive substrate and should not be presented as a conventional plastic finish.
- Film thickness affects threads, holes, press fits, sealing faces, and conductive contact areas; masking or secondary operations may be required.
- Blind holes, enclosed cavities, lap joints, and complex assemblies can create drainage, venting, or coating-buildup issues.
- Rack points must maintain electrical contact and can leave local contact marks, so their location should be defined in advance.
- Corrosion, color, appearance, and weathering performance depend on the specific pretreatment, coating chemistry, curing, and service environment.

## H2：常见问题 / Frequently Asked Questions

### 电泳涂装用于什么？ / What Is Electrophoretic Coating Used For?

它主要用于导电金属零件的均匀涂层、防腐基础保护和后续涂装底层，特别适合需要覆盖边角、孔位周围、凹槽或部分内腔的结构。

It is mainly used for uniform coating, baseline corrosion protection, and primer support on conductive metal parts, especially where edges, areas around holes, grooves, or some cavities require coverage.

### E-coating 可以用于塑料吗？ / Can E-coating Be Used on Plastic?

普通 E-coating 依赖导电基材和电沉积过程，因此不应默认用于塑料。除非项目存在经过确认的专用导电化和涂层方案，否则应把它作为金属表面处理讨论。

Conventional E-coating depends on a conductive substrate and electrodeposition, so it should not be assumed suitable for plastic. It should be treated as a metal finishing process unless the project has a separately confirmed conductive preparation and coating route.

### 哪些金属可以进行电泳涂装？ / Which Metals Can Be E-coated?

钢和铝是常见评估对象。镀锌钢及其他导电金属需要按材料状态、前处理、导电性、附着力、固化和使用环境确认。

Steel and aluminum are common candidates. Galvanized steel and other conductive metals require confirmation based on material condition, pretreatment, conductivity, adhesion, curing, and service environment.

### 电泳涂装会影响尺寸吗？ / Does Electrophoretic Coating Affect Dimensions?

会。涂层会增加膜厚，可能影响螺纹、孔、密封面、压配合和导电接触面。应在图纸和询价资料中标注这些区域，并确认遮蔽或后加工要求。

Yes. The coating adds film thickness and can affect threads, holes, sealing faces, press fits, and conductive contact areas. Identify these zones in drawings and RFQ documents and confirm masking or secondary-operation requirements.

### E-coating 与粉末喷涂相同吗？ / Is E-coating the Same as Powder Coating?

不同。E-coating 通过浸没和电沉积形成涂层；粉末喷涂通过静电施粉并固化形成表面。两者可分别使用，也可在经验证的涂层体系中把 E-coating 作为粉末喷涂底层。

No. E-coating uses immersion and electrodeposition, while powder coating applies electrostatically charged powder before curing. They can be used separately or, in a validated coating stack, with E-coating as a primer beneath powder coating.

### 电泳涂装能覆盖内腔吗？ / Can E-coating Cover Internal Cavities?

它通常比仅靠喷涂更容易覆盖部分内腔和凹陷，但最终结果仍受开口、排液、排气、电场分布、挂具和几何限制影响。完全封闭区域不能假设能够获得有效覆盖。

It can often cover some cavities and recesses more effectively than spray-only application, but openings, drainage, venting, electric-field distribution, racking, and geometry still affect the result. Effective coverage should not be assumed in fully enclosed areas.

### 如何确定防腐性能？ / How Is Corrosion Performance Defined?

应根据基材、前处理、涂层体系、膜厚、固化、零件几何和使用环境设定测试方法与验收标准。不能仅用“E-coating”名称推断固定的耐腐蚀小时数。

Define the test method and acceptance criteria based on substrate, pretreatment, coating chemistry, film thickness, curing, part geometry, and service environment. A fixed corrosion-resistance duration should not be inferred from the term “E-coating” alone.
