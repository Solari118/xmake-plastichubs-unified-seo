# Blog Topic Map — PlasticHubs

## 1. Blog Strategy

Blog content should support conversion pages instead of becoming an isolated education library. Every article should link upward to one or more P1/P2 pages: Plastic CNC Machining, Quote/RFQ, Material Hub, Rapid Prototyping, Low-Volume Production, Vacuum Casting, Injection Molding, Quality/DFM, and Industry pages.

## 3. Blog Build Order

1. Process comparison articles: CNC vs 3D printing, CNC vs injection molding, small-batch manufacturing options.
2. Material selection guides: best plastics, POM vs Nylon, PEEK vs POM.
3. RFQ and DFM support: cost factors, tolerance guide, CAD/RFQ checklist.
4. Industry guides: medical, automotive, IoT, robotics.
5. Glossary: what is CNC plastic machining, material definitions.

## 4. Avoid

- Do not create generic “what is manufacturing” articles.
- Do not target metal-heavy manufacturing topics unless connected to plastic assemblies or process comparison.
- Do not publish material comparison blogs without linking to material service pages and quote CTAs.
