# **SECTION 1: Company Identity & Brand Narrative**

## **1.1 Who We Are**

XMAKE is an intelligent on-demand manufacturing platform founded in 2010
and headquartered in Shenzhen, China. For engineers and sourcing
managers outside China, XMAKE is the single access point to precision
manufacturing --- combining 8 self-operated factories with a 1,000+
audited supplier network. We deliver custom parts from functional
prototypes to mass production runs for clients across 70+ countries,
without requiring them to understand or navigate China\'s supply chain
directly.

third parties, XMAKE owns and operates its core manufacturing assets.
Every order benefits from in-house engineering oversight, real-time DFM
analysis, and end-to-end quality control --- from raw material
