## H2：什么是水转印 / What Is Water Transfer Printing

水转印也称 hydro dipping 或 hydrographics，是一种把印刷图案转移到已完成预处理和底涂的零件表面的装饰工艺。图案膜铺在水面并经过活化，零件以受控角度穿过膜层，使图案包覆到外表面。随后通过冲洗、干燥和透明面漆完成表面处理。

与平面印刷或单一标识不同，water transfer printing 适合许多曲面、轮廓面和复杂外形。它不能自动消除所有接缝、拉伸或局部变形；深凹、尖角、倒扣和封闭区域需要提前评估。

Water transfer printing, also called hydro dipping or hydrographics, is a decorative process that transfers a printed pattern onto a prepared and base-coated part. The film is placed on water and activated, and the part passes through it at a controlled angle so the pattern wraps around the exposed surface. Rinsing, drying, and clear coating complete the finish.

Unlike flat printing or a single printed mark, water transfer printing can cover many curves, contours, and complex shapes. It does not automatically eliminate every seam, stretch, or local distortion; deep recesses, sharp edges, undercuts, and enclosed areas require advance review.

## H2：兼容材料 / Compatible Materials

### 铝 / Aluminum

建议工艺组合：表面预处理、底色漆、转印膜和透明面漆。基材处理和底涂附着力会影响最终稳定性。

Recommended setup: Surface preparation, base coat, transfer film, and clear topcoat. Substrate treatment and base-coat adhesion affect finish stability.

### 钢 / Steel

建议工艺组合：清洁或前处理、适配的底涂、转印膜和保护性面漆。防腐要求应由完整涂层体系承担，不能只依赖图案膜。

Recommended setup: Cleaning or pretreatment, a compatible base coat, transfer film, and protective topcoat. Corrosion requirements must be addressed by the complete coating system, not the printed pattern alone.

### 特定塑料 / Selected Plastics

需要检查材料类型、脱模剂残留、表面能、耐溶剂性、底涂和固化条件。不能假设所有塑料都适用同一套涂层体系。

Material type, mold-release residue, surface energy, solvent sensitivity, base coat, and curing conditions require review. One coating system should not be assumed suitable for every plastic.

### 已喷涂或已底涂表面 / Painted or Primed Surfaces

需要确认现有涂层的附着力、兼容性、颜色一致性和再涂窗口。未经确认的旧涂层可能导致剥离、起皱或颜色偏差。

Existing coating adhesion, compatibility, color consistency, and recoat window require confirmation. An unverified prior coating can contribute to peeling, wrinkling, or color variation.

## H2：Xmake 水转印流程 / Our Water Transfer Printing Process

1. **零件评审 / Part Review**：确认材料、几何、主视面、装配面、边角、凹陷和遮蔽区域。
2. **图案与涂层定义 / Pattern and Coating Definition**：确定底色、图案膜、图案方向、面漆光泽和使用环境。
3. **表面预处理 / Surface Preparation**：根据基材完成清洁、打磨或其他适配的预处理，并建立可附着的底涂表面。
4. **底涂与转印准备 / Base Coating and Transfer Setup**：施加底色漆，准备图案膜、入水方向和工装方案。
5. **活化与转印 / Activation and Transfer**：活化膜层，并以受控角度和速度完成图案转移。
6. **冲洗与干燥 / Rinsing and Drying**：去除残余膜材并充分干燥，避免影响后续面漆。
7. **透明面漆 / Clear Coating**：按光泽、触感和使用条件施加适配的透明面漆。
8. **外观检查与包装 / Cosmetic Inspection and Packaging**：检查图案连续性、颜色、光泽、遮蔽边界和关键外观面，再进行保护性包装。

## H2：常见问题 / Frequently Asked Questions

### 哪些零件适合水转印？ / What Parts Are Suitable for Water Transfer Printing?

许多曲面外壳、盖板、手柄、面板和装饰件可以评估水转印。适用性取决于材料、表面状态、几何、底涂、图案膜和面漆要求。

Many curved housings, covers, handles, panels, and decorative parts can be reviewed for water transfer printing. Suitability depends on material, surface condition, geometry, base coat, film, and topcoat requirements.

### 水转印可以用于塑料和金属吗？ / Can Water Transfer Printing Be Used on Plastic and Metal?

可以用于特定塑料和金属，但不能假设所有材料都兼容。塑料需要评估表面能、耐溶剂性和固化条件；金属需要适当的前处理和底涂。

It can be used on selected plastics and metals, but universal compatibility should not be assumed. Plastics require review of surface energy, solvent sensitivity, and curing conditions; metals require suitable pretreatment and base coating.

### 图案会在曲面上变形吗？ / Will the Pattern Distort on Curved Surfaces?

可能会。曲率、入水方向、图案比例、凹陷、尖角和倒扣都会影响拉伸和接缝。关键外观件应先评估几何并批准样件。

It can. Curvature, dipping direction, pattern scale, recesses, sharp edges, and undercuts affect stretch and seams. Geometry review and sample approval are appropriate for appearance-critical parts.

### 碳纤维风格水转印是否能增强零件？ / Does Carbon Fiber Style Hydro Dipping Strengthen a Part?

不能。它只是模拟编织碳纤维的视觉图案，不改变基材结构，也不能替代碳纤维复合材料。

No. It is a visual pattern that imitates woven carbon fiber. It does not change the substrate structure or replace a carbon fiber composite.

### 水转印是否防刮或防腐？ / Is Water Transfer Printing Scratch-Resistant or Corrosion-Resistant?

图案膜主要负责装饰。耐刮、耐候、耐化学和防腐表现取决于基材前处理、底涂、透明面漆、膜厚和使用环境，需要按项目确认。

The transferred pattern is mainly decorative. Scratch, weathering, chemical, and corrosion performance depend on substrate preparation, base coat, clear topcoat, film thickness, and service environment and must be confirmed for the project.

### 水转印会影响尺寸吗？ / Does Water Transfer Printing Affect Dimensions?

会增加涂层厚度。螺纹、孔、密封面、压配合和精密接触区域应在图纸或询价资料中标注，并根据需要遮蔽。

It adds coating thickness. Threads, holes, sealing faces, press fits, and precision contact areas should be identified in the drawing or RFQ and masked where needed.

### 询价需要提供什么？ / What Should I Include in an RFQ?
