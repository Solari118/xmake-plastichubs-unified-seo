## H2：What Is CNC Engraving

CNC engraving 是一种受程序控制的材料去除型标记工艺。小直径刀具或尖头雕刻刀沿文字、线条和图形路径移动，在零件表面切出浅槽、轮廓或局部凹区。最终结果是基材中的几何特征，而不是附着在表面的印刷层。

该工艺适合零件编号、序列号、logo、方向标识、刻度和需要触感的标记。它属于 Printing & Marking 类别，但与 silk screen printing、pad printing 等加墨工艺不同；它也不同于 laser engraving，后者使用光束改变或去除表层。

可读性不能只看字体名称。刀尖直径与夹角、字符间距、线宽、深度、表面曲率、材料颜色和观察角度都会改变结果。RFQ 应提供实际尺寸图稿、矢量文件、工程图或明确的标记区域。

## H2：Advantages & Notes

### Advantages of CNC Engraving

- Direct part identification：标记被加工进基材，不依赖可能脱落的标签或仅附着在表面的油墨。
- Datum-based placement：编程刀路可使字符和图形与孔、边缘、中心线或设计基准建立明确的位置关系。

- Manufacturing integration：雕刻可纳入零件加工路线，同时规划基准、装夹和后续 finish。

### Important Notes

- Fine detail has physical limits：最小线宽、内角和字符尺寸受刀尖直径、夹角、跳动、深度及材料影响，不能只按屏幕图稿判断。

- Contrast is not automatic：裸材上的浅雕刻可能主要依靠阴影形成对比；填色、coating 或 finish 后显色需单独确认。
- Curved surfaces need planning：曲面、斜面和圆周标记需要稳定装夹、刀具可达性和表面补偿，不能直接套用平面刀路。
- Process order changes the outcome：finish 前雕刻可能被后续膜层覆盖或钝化；finish 后雕刻可能暴露基材并中断局部保护层。

## H2：Frequently Asked Questions

### CNC engraving 通常用于什么？

常见用途包括零件编号、序列号、logo、方向符号、控制面板文字、刻度和触感标记。方案应根据追溯、操作、品牌识别或装饰目的选择。

### CNC engraving 与 laser engraving 相同吗？

不同。CNC engraving 使用刀具接触零件并去除材料；laser engraving 或 laser marking 使用光束改变表面。若项目重视凹入深度、机械切削或相对于加工基准的位置关系，可评估 CNC engraving；若重视极细表面标记或加工节拍，应另行比较 laser 工艺。

### 哪些材料可以进行 CNC engraving？

许多可加工金属和塑料可以评估，但不能概括为所有材料。请提供具体牌号、硬度或状态、表面 finish 和目标深度，用于判断刀具、毛刺、热、开裂及对比度风险。

### CNC engraving 会影响零件尺寸吗？

它会在标记区域去除材料。未切削区域通常不变，但标记靠近薄壁、密封面、配合面或承载特征时，局部几何变化可能影响功能，应在图纸中定义避让区或提出工程评估要求。

### 可以在曲面或圆周面上雕刻吗？

部分曲面和圆周面可以评估，但结果取决于机床运动、刀具方向、表面曲率、装夹和标记范围。不能把平面图稿直接投射到曲面后，假定线宽和深度保持不变。

### CNC engraving 能做多小的文字？

没有适用于所有材料和刀具的统一最小尺寸。字符高度、笔画宽度、间距、深度、刀尖几何和材料共同决定可读性。应提交实际尺寸图稿进行工艺评估，而不是只指定字体名称。

### 应该在 anodizing 或 coating 之前还是之后雕刻？

两种顺序会产生不同结果。finish 前雕刻可能被后续膜层覆盖、变钝或改变颜色；finish 后雕刻可形成基材对比，但也会切穿局部保护层。选择应结合外观、腐蚀、磨损和尺寸要求。

### 报价需要提供哪些资料？

提供 3D CAD、2D 图纸、材料牌号、标记内容、矢量 logo 或字体文件、位置基准、目标深度、可变编号规则、外观要求和后续 finish。Xmake 将据此评估刀具可达性、装夹、工序顺序和待确认的质量要求。

## H2: What Is CNC Engraving

CNC engraving is a programmed, material-removal marking process. A small-diameter tool or pointed engraving cutter follows text, line, and graphic paths to cut shallow grooves, outlines, or local recesses into the part surface. The result is a geometric feature in the substrate, not a printed layer attached to it.

The process is used for part numbers, serial numbers, logos, direction marks, graduations, and markings that need a tactile profile. It sits within the Printing & Marking category but differs from ink-transfer processes such as silk screen and pad printing. It also differs from laser engraving, which uses a beam to alter or remove the surface layer.

Legibility depends on more than a font name. Cutter-tip diameter and angle, character spacing, stroke width, depth, surface curvature, material color, and viewing angle all affect the result. An RFQ should include artwork at actual size, a vector file, an engineering drawing, or a clearly defined marking zone.

## H2: Advantages & Notes

### Advantages of CNC Engraving

- Direct part identification: The mark is machined into the substrate and does not depend on a label that may detach or ink applied only to the surface.
- Datum-based placement: A programmed toolpath can relate text and graphics to holes, edges, centerlines, or design datums.
- Tactile result: Recessed profiles can create finger-detectable direction points, graduations, or identification features.
- Manufacturing integration: Engraving can be included in the part-routing plan together with datums, fixturing, and downstream finishes.

- Fine detail has physical limits: Minimum stroke width, internal corners, and character size depend on tip diameter, included angle, runout, depth, and material. Screen artwork alone is not enough to judge feasibility.

- Contrast is not automatic: Shallow engraving on bare material may rely mainly on shadow contrast. Color fill, coating, or post-finish contrast requires separate confirmation.
- Curved surfaces need planning: Curves, slopes, and circumferential marks require stable fixturing, cutter access, and surface compensation. A flat toolpath cannot be applied unchanged.
- Process order changes the outcome: Engraving before finishing may be covered or softened by a later layer, while engraving after finishing may expose the substrate and interrupt local protection.

## H2: Frequently Asked Questions

### What is CNC engraving commonly used for?

Common uses include part numbers, serial numbers, logos, direction symbols, control-panel text, graduations, and tactile marks. The approach should match the purpose: traceability, operation, branding, or decoration.

### Is CNC engraving the same as laser engraving?

No. CNC engraving uses a cutter that contacts the part and removes material; laser engraving or laser marking uses a beam to alter the surface. CNC engraving is worth evaluating when recessed depth, mechanical cutting, or position relative to machining datums matters. Compare laser processes separately when very fine surface marks or cycle time is the priority.

### Which materials can be CNC engraved?

Many machinable metals and plastics can be evaluated, but not every material should be assumed suitable. Provide the exact grade, hardness or condition, surface finish, and target depth so cutter, burr, heat, cracking, and contrast risks can be reviewed.

### Does CNC engraving affect part dimensions?

It removes material within the marking zone. Areas outside the cut are normally unchanged, but local geometry can affect function when a mark sits near a thin wall, sealing face, fit, or load-bearing feature. Define a keep-out zone or request engineering review on the drawing.

### Can CNC engraving be applied to curved or circumferential surfaces?

Selected curved and circumferential surfaces can be evaluated, but the result depends on machine motion, cutter orientation, surface curvature, fixturing, and marking range. Do not project flat artwork onto a curve and assume stroke width and depth will remain unchanged.

### How small can CNC-engraved text be?

There is no single minimum that applies to every material and cutter. Character height, stroke width, spacing, depth, tip geometry, and material determine legibility together. Submit the artwork at actual size for process review instead of specifying only a font name.

### Should engraving be done before or after anodizing or coating?

The two sequences produce different results. Engraving before finishing may be covered, softened, or recolored by the later layer. Engraving after finishing can expose contrasting substrate but also interrupts the local protective layer. Choose the sequence according to appearance, corrosion, wear, and dimensional requirements.

### What information is needed for a quotation?

Provide the 3D CAD model, 2D drawing, material grade, marking content, vector logo or font file, location datums, target depth, variable-number rules, cosmetic requirements, and downstream finish. Xmake will use these inputs to review cutter access, fixturing, operation sequence, and quality requirements that still need confirmation.
