## 4. Internal Linking Notes

- Every material page should link to: Plastic CNC Machining, Plastic CNC Machining Quote, relevant industries and material comparison blogs.
- Every process page should link to a comparison guide to help buyers self-select.
- Every P1 service page should include links to Quality Control, Certifications, DFM Review, Tolerances and Quote/RFQ.
- Every industry page should link to the most relevant materials and processes.
- Every blog should have at least one commercial CTA and at least two internal links to P1/P2 pages.
- Quote/RFQ page should be linked globally from header, service pages, material pages, comparison pages and quality pages.

## 5. Content Gaps

- Stronger supplier/quote intent pages are needed: “plastic CNC machining supplier,” “custom plastic parts quote,” “online custom plastic parts service.”
- Material pages need a hub structure rather than scattered standalone pages.
- Process decision content is needed to explain when CNC, 3D printing, vacuum casting and injection molding are the right choice.
- Quality and certification pages should be linked from medical/automotive pages.
- Case studies should be created as soon as real projects are available: medical PEEK part, POM bushing/gear, acrylic display/optical part, IoT enclosure, robotics component.

## 6. Duplicate Or Cannibalization Risks

| Risk | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| “plastic CNC machining” vs “custom plastic machining” | Use the first as core service; use the second as supplier/commercial page or section with distinct intent |
| “plastic prototyping” vs “rapid prototyping” | Use one parent page and supporting sections unless SERP shows separate strong intent |
| “plastic 3D printing” vs “rapid prototyping” | Keep 3D printing as process page; rapid prototyping as solution page |
| “low-volume production” vs “low-volume CNC machining” | Use low-volume production as solution page; low-volume CNC machining as subpage/section under CNC |
| “material guide” vs material pages | Material guide is hub/comparison; each material page should target service + quote intent |
