Fixed artwork, flat metal parts, and clear spot colors are a typical screen-printing combination. Compare laser marking or digital printing for variable data, pad printing for small compound curves, and CNC or laser engraving when the mark must be recessed into the substrate.

## H2：丝网印刷如何把图稿转移到金属上 / How Screen Printing Transfers Artwork to Metal

网框上的张紧网布被感光材料制成图文模板，图形区域保持开放。刮板推动油墨通过开放网孔，油墨在网版离开后留在金属表面，随后按油墨体系进行挥发干燥、化学交联、热固化或 UV 固化。

“Silk screen” 是沿用至今的工艺名称，不表示现代网版必须使用天然丝。实际网布、目数、张力、乳剂厚度、离网距离和刮板条件会共同影响墨层厚度、边缘清晰度和细节再现。

A tensioned mesh on a frame is converted into an image stencil with open graphic areas. A squeegee drives ink through those openings; the ink remains on the metal after the screen lifts and is then dried by solvent evaporation, chemical crosslinking, thermal cure, or UV cure according to the ink system.

“Silk screen” is the established process name and does not mean a modern screen must use natural silk. Mesh type and count, tension, emulsion thickness, off-contact distance, and squeegee conditions work together to control ink deposit, edge definition, and detail reproduction.

## H2：先写清表面状态，再写金属名称 / Specify Surface Condition Before Metal Name

“铝”或“不锈钢”不足以完成油墨选型。同一种铝件可能是裸铝、阳极氧化、喷粉、湿式喷漆或镀层表面，这些界面的润湿、溶剂敏感性、表面能和固化耐温完全不同。

“Aluminum” or “stainless steel” is not enough for ink selection. The same aluminum part may be bare, anodized, powder coated, wet painted, or plated, and each interface has different wetting, solvent sensitivity, surface energy, and cure-temperature limits.

| 实际印刷表面 | 主要风险 | 报价前需要的信息 |
|---|---|---|
| 裸铝 | 氧化层、加工油、指纹、表面粗糙度和批次状态影响润湿与附着 | 合金、加工状态、清洗、外观等级和是否需要前处理 |
| 阳极氧化铝 | 封孔状态、染色、光泽和表面处理供应批次会改变附着 | 阳极氧化类型、颜色、封孔、现有规范和实际样件 |
| 不锈钢 | 油污、钝化膜、抛光膏和低表面能污染物可能导致缩墨或脱落 | 牌号、表面等级、钝化 / 抛光状态和清洁限制 |
| 涂装金属 | 实际接触面是粉末涂层或油漆，不是下面的金属 | 涂层树脂类型、固化状态、光泽、添加剂、重涂窗口和耐温 |
| 镀层金属 | 镀层化学、孔隙、保护膜和防指纹处理会改变油墨结合 | 镀层类型、后处理、清洁限制和样件测试 |

| Actual Printing Surface | Main Risk | Information Needed Before Quotation |
|---|---|---|
| Bare aluminum | Oxide, machining oil, fingerprints, roughness, and batch condition affect wetting and adhesion | Alloy, manufacturing condition, cleaning, appearance grade, and pretreatment need |
| Anodized aluminum | Sealing, dye, gloss, and finishing batches can change adhesion | Anodizing type, color, sealing, existing specification, and actual sample |
| Stainless steel | Oil, passive film, polishing compound, and low-surface-energy contamination can cause crawl or release | Grade, surface finish, passivation/polishing state, and cleaning restrictions |
| Coated metal | The ink contacts powder coat or paint, not the metal below | Coating resin, cure state, gloss, additives, recoat window, and temperature limit |
| Plated metal | Plating chemistry, pores, protective films, and anti-fingerprint treatments alter bonding | Plating type, post-treatment, cleaning restrictions, and sample testing |

表面准备必须与零件状态相匹配。除油、除尘、去除指纹或经验证的表面活化可能有帮助，但未经确认的强溶剂、打磨或火焰处理也可能损伤阳极层、漆膜或外观面。

Surface preparation must match the part condition. Degreasing, dust removal, fingerprint removal, or a validated activation step may help, while an unapproved aggressive solvent, abrasion, or flame treatment can damage anodizing, paint, or a cosmetic surface.

## H2：从矢量图稿到可印刷色版 / From Vector Artwork to Printable Separations

### 矢量主文件 / Vector Master

文字、线条、Logo 和刻度应优先提供可编辑矢量文件，并把字体转曲或同时提供受控字体。仅提供低分辨率截图会带来边缘锯齿、笔画变化和尺寸解释问题。

Text, lines, logos, and graduations should be supplied as an editable vector master, with fonts outlined or provided as controlled font files. A low-resolution screenshot introduces jagged edges, stroke changes, and ambiguous dimensions.

### 每色独立分色 / One Separation per Color

传统专色丝网印刷通常为每一种颜色制作独立色版，并逐色套印。颜色数量增加会带来更多网版、固化步骤和套准累积，图稿应明确叠印、镂空、底白和颜色顺序。

Traditional spot-color screen printing normally uses a separate stencil for each color and registers the colors in sequence. More colors add screens, cure steps, and cumulative registration variation, so artwork must define overprints, knockouts, white underbase, and print order.

### 深色底材上的遮盖 / Opacity on Dark Substrates

深色阳极氧化、黑色喷粉或彩色漆面会改变油墨视觉结果。浅色图形可能需要高遮盖配方、底白或多次印刷；增加墨层也会影响细节、固化和边缘，因此应以实际底材样件确认。

Dark anodizing, black powder coat, or colored paint changes the visual result. Light graphics may require a high-opacity formulation, white underbase, or multiple print passes; added ink deposit also affects detail, cure, and edges, so the actual substrate must be sampled.

### 尺寸由图纸控制 / Dimensions Controlled by the Drawing

印刷位置不能只靠效果图。图纸应给出图形相对于孔、边缘、中心线或装配基准的位置，并标明主视方向、允许偏移、禁印区和裁切边界。

Print position should not rely on a rendering alone. The drawing should locate the graphic from holes, edges, centerlines, or assembly datums and define viewing direction, allowed shift, no-print zones, and trim boundaries.

## H2：四类金属丝印项目 / Four Metal Screen-Printing Programs

### 控制面板与操作文字 / Control-Panel Legends

适用于按钮名称、端口文字、警示符号、方向箭头和刻度。设计重点不是把字体做得尽可能小，而是保证安装方向、操作距离和最终表面反光条件下可读。

典型零件：铝面板 · 不锈钢面板 · 喷粉钣金件  
核心输入：字体 / 符号 · 位置基准 · 颜色 · 清洁剂 · 可见面

Used for button names, port labels, warnings, direction arrows, and graduations. The design goal is not the smallest possible type; it is legibility at the installed orientation, operating distance, and final surface reflectance.

Typical Parts: Aluminum panels · Stainless-steel panels · Powder-coated sheet-metal parts  
Core Inputs: Type/symbol · Position datum · Color · Cleaner · Visible surface

### Logo 与品牌图形 / Logos and Brand Graphics

Logo 印刷需要同时控制矢量轮廓、专色、底材颜色和商标留白区。金属色、荧光色或特殊效果油墨的视觉结果与普通专色不同，应先确认材料可得性、样板和批次接受方式。

典型零件：外壳 · 铭牌 · 盖板 · 装饰面板  
核心输入：矢量 Logo · 色样 · 底材 · 光泽 · 观察条件

Logo printing requires control of vector outlines, spot color, substrate color, and clear space. Metallic, fluorescent, or other effect inks look different from standard spot colors and require availability, sample, and batch-acceptance review.

Typical Parts: Housings · Nameplates · Covers · Decorative panels  
Core Inputs: Vector logo · Color sample · Substrate · Gloss · Viewing condition

### 多色套印图形 / Registered Multicolor Graphics

多色项目按颜色顺序逐次印刷和固化，套准误差会在细边、反白文字和相邻色块处最明显。图稿可以使用合理的 trapping 或间隙设计降低视觉风险，但具体补偿需要结合颜色顺序和油墨流变确认。

典型零件：人机界面面板 · 多语言操作面板 · 彩色标识  
核心输入：分色文件 · 套准容差 · 印刷顺序 · 底白 · 首件样

Multicolor work is printed and cured in sequence, making registration variation most visible around fine borders, reversed text, and adjacent color blocks. Practical trapping or clearance can reduce visual risk, but compensation must match print order and ink rheology.

Typical Parts: HMI panels · Multilingual operator panels · Color-coded signs  
Core Inputs: Separations · Registration tolerance · Print order · White underbase · First article

### 功能符号与工艺标记 / Functional Symbols and Process Marks

丝印可用于固定的极性、接地、连接、方向、量程或安全图形。若标记承担法规、操作安全或追溯功能，内容、位置、耐久性和版本控制必须由项目文件明确，不能把普通外观检查当作完整验证。

典型零件：电气外壳 · 仪器面板 · 固定规格铭牌  
核心输入：受控符号 · 版本 · 使用环境 · 磨耗 / 清洁 · 法规要求

Screen printing can apply fixed polarity, grounding, connection, direction, range, or safety graphics. If the mark carries regulatory, operating-safety, or traceability meaning, content, position, durability, and revision control must be defined by project documents; cosmetic inspection alone is incomplete.

Typical Parts: Electrical housings · Instrument panels · Fixed-specification nameplates  
Core Inputs: Controlled symbol · Revision · Environment · Wear/cleaning · Regulatory requirement

## H2：油墨选择从使用环境反推 / Select Ink Backward from the Service Environment

| 使用条件 | 油墨与固化评审重点 | 不应直接假设 |
|---|---|---|
| 室内低频触碰 | 基材附着、颜色、完全固化和普通擦拭 | “室内”就不需要附着或清洁测试 |
| 频繁手触与按键区 | 皮脂、磨耗、摩擦抛光、清洁剂和图形位置 | 高光油墨一定比哑光更耐磨 |
| 户外暴露 | UV、温湿循环、雨水、底材涂层和颜色保持 | 所有颜色和所有油墨具有相同户外寿命 |
| 化学品或消毒清洁 | 介质名称、浓度、接触方式、停留时间和循环次数 | “耐酒精”代表耐所有溶剂或消毒剂 |
| 后续覆膜或透明层 | 层间附着、溶剂相容、颜色变化和总工艺窗口 | 透明层一定提高耐久且不会改变外观 |
| 热固化受限组件 | 零件、胶粘件、密封件和原有涂层的耐温 | 金属本体耐温就等于整个组件耐温 |

| Service Condition | Ink and Cure Review Focus | Do Not Assume |
|---|---|---|
| Indoor, infrequent touch | Substrate adhesion, color, full cure, and routine wiping | “Indoor” eliminates the need for adhesion or cleaning tests |
| Frequent hand contact or keypad zone | Skin oils, abrasion, friction polishing, cleaner, and graphic location | Gloss ink always wears better than matte ink |
| Outdoor exposure | UV, temperature/humidity cycles, rain, substrate coating, and color retention | Every color and ink has the same outdoor life |
| Chemical or disinfectant cleaning | Chemical name, concentration, contact mode, dwell time, and cycles | “Alcohol resistant” means resistant to every solvent or disinfectant |
| Laminate or clear overprint | Intercoat adhesion, solvent compatibility, color change, and total process window | A clear layer always improves durability without changing appearance |
| Heat-limited assembly | Temperature limit of the part, adhesives, seals, and existing coatings | Heat tolerance of the metal equals heat tolerance of the full assembly |

油墨系列必须通过技术数据表和实际基材样件选择。同一油墨在不同阳极氧化批次、粉末涂层树脂或防指纹表面上的表现可能不同；供应商列出“metal”也不能替代项目试印。

The ink series must be selected through its technical data sheet and an actual-substrate trial. One ink may behave differently across anodizing batches, powder-coat resins, or anti-fingerprint surfaces; a supplier listing “metal” does not replace a project print trial.

## H2：几何和工装决定能不能印到位 / Geometry and Tooling Determine Print Placement

### 平面零件 / Flat Parts

平板、面板和铭牌最容易建立稳定支撑、离网和位置基准。凸起螺柱、已装配五金、翘曲或邻近折弯会限制网框与刮板路径，即使印刷区域本身看起来是平的。

Flat plates, panels, and nameplates provide the clearest support, off-contact, and location datums. Raised studs, installed hardware, warpage, or nearby bends can obstruct the screen or squeegee path even when the print zone itself appears flat.

### 浅曲面 / Gently Curved Surfaces

部分单向缓曲面可以通过适配网版、工装和印刷行程评估。曲率会改变网版接触、墨层和图形尺寸，不应把平面图稿直接包覆后假定线宽与位置不变。

Some shallow, one-direction curves can be reviewed with suitable screen, tooling, and print travel. Curvature changes screen contact, ink deposit, and image dimensions; a flat graphic should not simply be wrapped around the surface with unchanged line width and position assumed.

### 圆柱与复合曲面 / Cylinders and Compound Curves

圆柱面可能需要专用旋转丝印设备和与圆周同步的工装。小面积复合曲面、凹面或多方向曲率通常更适合移印；选择取决于图形面积、曲率、位置和批量，而不是只看零件外形名称。

Cylinders may require dedicated rotary screen equipment and tooling synchronized to the circumference. Small compound curves, concave surfaces, or multi-directional curvature are often better suited to pad printing; the decision depends on graphic area, curvature, position, and volume rather than the part name alone.

## H2：印刷缺陷应按现象追原因 / Diagnose Print Defects from the Observed Failure

| 现象 | 优先检查 | 常见修正方向 |
|---|---|---|
| 缩墨、鱼眼或局部拒墨 | 油污、硅、指纹、表面能、清洁剂残留 | 纠正清洁和污染控制，验证表面活化与油墨润湿 |
| 边缘毛糙或锯齿 | 图稿质量、网目、乳剂、网版张力、离网和油墨流动 | 修正矢量文件，调整制版与印刷参数 |
| 细线断开或小字糊版 | 线宽、网孔开放、油墨黏度、刮板和网版释放 | 放宽细节或调整网版、油墨和刮印条件 |
| 套色偏移 | 定位基准、工装间隙、零件公差、网版伸长和固化顺序 | 改善基准与夹具，修正分色补偿和过程控制 |
| 擦拭掉色或划伤 | 基材附着、固化、墨层、清洁剂、磨耗载荷 | 更换或调整油墨体系，完成固化并按实际介质测试 |
| 色差或遮盖不足 | 底材颜色、墨层厚度、底白、批次和观察条件 | 用实际底材批准样件，控制配色、网版和印刷次数 |
| 气泡、针孔或表面不平 | 油墨混合、空气、底材粗糙、污染和固化挥发 | 调整混合与静置、印刷参数、表面准备和固化曲线 |

| Symptom | Check First | Common Correction Direction |
|---|---|---|
| Crawling, fisheyes, or local ink rejection | Oil, silicone, fingerprints, surface energy, and cleaner residue | Correct cleaning and contamination control; validate activation and ink wetting |
| Ragged or sawtooth edge | Artwork quality, mesh, emulsion, tension, off-contact, and ink flow | Correct vector artwork and adjust screen-making and print parameters |
| Broken fine line or filled small text | Line width, mesh opening, ink viscosity, squeegee, and screen release | Open the detail or adjust screen, ink, and print conditions |
| Color misregistration | Location datum, fixture clearance, part tolerance, screen stretch, and cure sequence | Improve datums and fixture; revise separation compensation and process control |
| Wipes off or scratches | Substrate adhesion, cure, ink deposit, cleaner, and abrasion load | Change or adjust the ink system, complete cure, and test with actual media |
| Color shift or poor opacity | Substrate color, ink deposit, underbase, batch, and viewing condition | Approve on the actual substrate and control color mix, screen, and print passes |
| Bubbles, pinholes, or uneven surface | Ink mixing, entrained air, substrate texture, contamination, and cure volatiles | Adjust mixing/rest, print parameters, surface preparation, and cure profile |

## H2：Xmake 金属丝网印刷流程 / Xmake Metal Screen-Printing Workflow

1. **图稿与版本核对 / Artwork and Revision Check**：确认矢量主文件、文字、Logo、颜色、分色、版本和受控符号。
2. **零件与表面确认 / Part and Surface Confirmation**：核对金属牌号、实际表面处理、印刷区域、基准、曲率和已装配特征。
3. **油墨与测试计划 / Ink and Test Plan**：根据颜色、清洁、磨耗、户外、化学和固化限制选择候选体系。
4. **分色与网版 / Separations and Screens**：建立每色图层、印刷顺序、套准补偿、底白和网版参数。
5. **工装与表面准备 / Tooling and Surface Preparation**：稳定零件位置，完成经验证的清洁，并保护非印刷区域。
6. **首件印刷 / First Article Print**：检查内容、方向、位置、颜色、遮盖、细节、套准和表面缺陷。
7. **逐色印刷与固化 / Sequential Printing and Curing**：按工艺顺序印刷各色，并控制中间干燥与最终固化。
8. **检验与保护包装 / Inspection and Protective Packaging**：完成项目测试，防止零件相互摩擦、堆叠压印或包装材料粘连图形。

## H2：丝网印刷与其他金属标记工艺 / Screen Printing vs Other Metal-Marking Processes

| 选择维度 | 丝网印刷 | 移印 | 激光打标 | 数字印刷 |

| 更适合的表面 | 平面和经评估的缓曲面 | 小面积复合曲面、凹凸面 | 光束可达且材料响应合适的表面 | 设备可处理的平面或专用三维工装 |
| 图形特点 | 专色、实色块、清晰文字和较高遮盖 | 小图形、局部符号、曲面转印 | 细小标识、序列号、二维码和基材对比 | 多色图像、短批和变量数据 |
| 生产准备 | 每色需要图稿分色、网版和套准 | 需要钢板 / 树脂版、胶头和工装 | 需要程序、参数和定位工装 | 需要色彩管理、文件和设备设置 |
| 耐久基础 | 取决于油墨-表面-固化组合 | 取决于油墨-表面-固化组合 | 取决于材料变化深度和后续环境 | 取决于油墨、底涂、固化和保护层 |
| 变量数据 | 不适合频繁逐件变化 | 不适合频繁逐件变化 | 通常适合 | 通常适合 |

## H2：验收不能只看第一眼 / Acceptance Must Cover More Than First Appearance

| 验收项目 | 应在采购资料中定义 | 可使用的确认方式 |
|---|---|---|
| 内容与版本 | 受控图稿、语言、符号、零件版本和变更记录 | 与批准 PDF / 矢量文件逐项核对 |

| 擦拭与耐化学 | 介质、浓度、擦拭材料、载荷、次数和判定 | 实际清洁剂或化学品循环测试 |
| 磨耗与触碰 | 接触物、载荷、路径、循环和允许变化 | 项目定义的摩擦 / 磨耗或模拟使用测试 |

| Acceptance Item | Define in Purchasing Documents | Possible Confirmation Method |
|---|---|---|
| Content and revision | Controlled artwork, language, symbols, part revision, and change record | Line-by-line comparison with approved PDF/vector artwork |
| Position and registration | Datums, orientation, location tolerance, color-to-color registration, and no-print zones | First-article measurement, transparent overlay, or project gauge |
| Color and opacity | Physical color sample, substrate, light source, gloss, and allowed difference | Same-substrate sample, controlled visual check, or instrumental color measurement |
| Adhesion | Actual surface, cure state, test timing, and rating requirement | Project-suitable ASTM D3359, ISO 2409, or approved method |
| Wipe and chemical resistance | Medium, concentration, wiping material, load, cycles, and acceptance | Actual-cleaner or chemical exposure cycle |
| Abrasion and touch | Contact material, load, path, cycles, and allowed change | Project-defined rub/abrasion or simulated-use test |

ASTM D3359-23 评估较有延展性的涂层在金属基材上经划切和胶带作用后的附着分级，不能给出绝对剥离强度。ISO 2409:2020 同样是划格抗分离评估；选择标准前应确认油墨膜、厚度、底材和项目规格是否在方法适用范围内。

ASTM D3359-23 rates relatively ductile coatings on metallic substrates after cutting and tape application; it does not provide an absolute peel-strength value. ISO 2409:2020 is likewise a cross-cut resistance-to-separation assessment. Before naming either standard, confirm that the ink film, thickness, substrate, and project specification fit the method scope.

## H2：常见问题 / Frequently Asked Questions

### 阳极氧化铝可以丝网印刷吗？ / Can Anodized Aluminum Be Screen Printed?

可以评估，但结果取决于阳极氧化类型、封孔、染色、表面污染和油墨体系。应使用实际量产表面试印；不能因为某一种阳极氧化样件通过，就推定所有颜色和供应批次都相同。

It can be evaluated, but the result depends on anodizing type, sealing, dye, contamination, and ink system. Print on the actual production surface; one successful anodized sample does not prove that every color and finishing batch will behave the same.

### 丝网印刷能做逐件变化的序列号吗？ / Can Screen Printing Produce Part-by-Part Serial Numbers?

技术上可以频繁更换网版或图稿，但通常不经济，也容易增加版本风险。逐件变量数据更适合激光打标或数字印刷，固定前缀和公共图形仍可保留丝印。

It is technically possible to change screens or artwork repeatedly, but it is usually uneconomical and increases revision risk. Laser marking or digital printing is better suited to part-by-part variable data, while a fixed prefix and common graphics can remain screen printed.

### 金属丝印需要透明保护层吗？ / Does Screen Printing on Metal Need a Clear Protective Layer?

不一定。适配并完全固化的油墨可能直接满足项目要求；透明层会增加一道材料界面，也可能改变颜色、光泽、附着和返修方式。是否使用应由磨耗、化学品、户外和外观测试决定。

Not always. A compatible, fully cured ink may meet the project requirements directly. A clear layer adds another material interface and can change color, gloss, adhesion, and repair behavior; use it only when wear, chemical, outdoor, and appearance testing support it.

### 最小文字和线宽是多少？ / What Is the Minimum Text or Line Width?

没有适用于所有网版、油墨和底材的统一最小值。字体笔画、阴阳字、网目、乳剂、墨层、底材粗糙度和套色数量共同决定可印性；应提交实际尺寸矢量图进行评估。

There is no universal minimum for every screen, ink, and substrate. Character stroke, positive or reversed text, mesh, emulsion, ink deposit, substrate roughness, and number of colors determine printability; submit full-size vector artwork for review.

### 询价需要提供哪些资料？ / What Should Be Included in an RFQ?

请提供 3D / 2D 零件文件、材料与实际表面处理、数量、矢量图稿、字体、颜色标准、印刷位置基准、套准要求、使用环境、清洁剂、磨耗条件和适用测试。若已有量产表面样件、色板或失效样，请一并提供。

Provide 3D/2D part files, material and actual surface finish, quantity, vector artwork, fonts, color standard, print-location datums, registration requirement, service environment, cleaner, wear condition, and applicable tests. Include production-finish samples, color standards, or failed parts when available.
