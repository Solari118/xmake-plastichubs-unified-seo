## H2：What is Tumbling Deburring

Tumbling deburring 是 mass finishing 的一种应用。零件与磨料介质或抛光介质一起放入滚筒、振动槽或振动盘中；设备产生旋转或振动后，零件、介质和 compound 之间形成相对运动，持续处理外露边缘和表面。

行业里常把 barrel tumbling、vibratory finishing 和 vibratory deburring 放在同一类讨论，但它们的运动方式和处理强度并不完全相同。滚筒方式通常适合小型、相对结实的零件和渐进式处理；振动式设备更便于观察和调整过程，也可处理多种批量零件。最终应按零件而不是按工艺名称选设备。

去毛刺与倒圆也不是同一个验收目标。Deburring 关注去掉凸出的薄边或残留物，同时尽量少动主体边缘；edge radiusing 则有意把边缘加工成更明显、较一致的圆弧。若图纸只写“break all sharp edges”，还应补充允许的边缘范围或样件标准。

## H2: What Is Tumbling Deburring

Tumbling deburring is an application of mass finishing. Parts are placed with abrasive or polishing media in a rotating barrel, vibratory tub, or vibratory bowl. Rotation or vibration creates relative motion among the parts, media, and compound, progressively working exposed edges and surfaces.

Barrel tumbling, vibratory finishing, and vibratory deburring are often discussed together, but they do not create identical motion or cutting action. Rotary barrels are commonly considered for small, reasonably robust parts and gradual finishing. Vibratory equipment offers a process that can be observed and adjusted while handling many batch-finishing tasks. Equipment should be selected around the part, not the label alone.

Burr removal and edge radiusing are also different acceptance targets. Deburring removes a protruding feather or remnant while limiting changes to the parent edge. Edge radiusing deliberately creates a more visible and consistent rounded edge. If a drawing says only “break all sharp edges,” add an allowable edge range or approved sample.

## H2: Types of Tumbling Deburring We Offer

A tumbling process should start with the defect or edge condition that must change. Removing a thin burr, creating a defined radius, lowering surface peaks, and producing a brighter finish require different media and process windows.

### H3: Light Burr Removal

Used for minor burrs and sharp projections left by CNC machining, turning, stamping, sawing, or small-part casting. The burr thickness, root, and direction should be reviewed first. A thick, firmly attached burr may bend over instead of separating cleanly in a tumbling process.

Target Result: Burr removal · Light edge break · Batch consistency

Key Inputs: Burr location · Burr severity · Material · Protected features

### H3: Controlled Edge Radiusing

When a part needs more than a light edge break, media cutting action and cycle time can be developed to create a deliberate edge radius. The process acts on multiple reachable edges at once, so it is not suited to one isolated edge unless the remaining features can be protected.

Target Result: Rounded exposed edges · Reduced sharpness · More uniform handling

Key Inputs: Target edge condition · Critical edges · Wall thickness · Sample standard

### H3: Surface Smoothing and Blending

Fine abrasive media can reduce light tool marks, shallow scratches, and local surface peaks so the batch has a more continuous appearance. It will not automatically remove deep cutter marks, pits, or geometric defects. Functional surfaces with an Ra requirement need sample processing and measurement.

Target Result: Smoothed peaks · Blended appearance · Pre-finish preparation

Key Inputs: Starting surface · Cosmetic zones · Roughness requirement · Downstream finish

### H3: Burnishing and Pre-polish Finishing

Low-cutting or non-cutting media can be evaluated for burnishing, brightening, or preparation before a separate polishing step. Brightness depends on the substrate, starting condition, media, compound, cleaning, and drying; tumbling should not be treated as an automatic mirror-polishing process.

Target Result: Brighter surface · Reduced fine texture · Pre-polish condition

Key Inputs: Material · Initial finish · Brightness target · Cleanliness requirement

Quick Selection Guide - Start with light burr removal for thin protruding burrs; use controlled edge radiusing when the edge must be intentionally softened; choose surface smoothing when batch appearance needs to be more uniform; and evaluate burnishing only when brightness or preparation for fine polishing is the target. Heavy burrs, thin walls, precision threads, and parts that permit work on only one local edge should first be compared with directional deburring, brushing, or manual treatment.

### Advantages of Tumbling Deburring

· Batch processing: 一次处理多件小型或中型零件，适合重复性的批量去毛刺任务

· Multi-edge contact: 可同时接触多个外露边缘和表面，减少逐边手工处理

· Adjustable finish direction: 通过介质、compound 与 cycle 调整去毛刺、倒圆、平滑或 brightening 的侧重点

· Useful process handoff: 可作为 machining、stamping 或 casting 与清洗、coating、plating 或装配之间的一道受控工序

### Important Notes

· The process is not selective: 能接触到的边缘通常会一起被处理；需要保持锐利的定位边、密封边和功能边必须提前标识

· Part-on-part contact can leave marks: 薄壁件、精密外观件和柔软合金可能碰伤、叠压或变形，应评估装载量、隔离处理或其他方式

· Media can lodge in features: 孔、窄槽、螺纹和盲腔可能卡住介质；介质尺寸与形状必须避开危险组合

· Heavy burrs may need pre-removal: 较厚、延展性强或方向明显的毛刺可能折弯而非断开，需要先刷磨、切削或局部去除

· Cleaning and drying are part of the result: 残留 abrasive、compound 或水分会影响后续工序；铁基零件还应考虑干燥和暂时性防锈

## H2: Advantage & Notes

### Advantages of Tumbling Deburring

· Batch processing: Multiple small or medium parts can be processed together for repeatable deburring work

· Multi-edge contact: Several exposed edges and surfaces can be worked in one cycle, reducing edge-by-edge manual effort

· Adjustable finish direction: Media, compound, and cycle can shift the emphasis among deburring, radiusing, smoothing, and brightening

· Useful process handoff: Tumbling can form a controlled step between machining, stamping, or casting and later cleaning, coating, plating, or assembly

### Important Notes

· The process is not selective: Reachable edges are generally worked together; locating edges, sealing edges, and functional edges that must stay sharp need to be identified in advance

· Part-on-part contact can leave marks: Thin walls, precision cosmetic parts, and soft alloys may nick, overlap, or distort; load, compartmental processing, or another method should be reviewed

· Media can lodge in features: Holes, narrow slots, threads, and blind cavities can retain media; media size and shape must avoid risky combinations

· Heavy burrs may need pre-removal: Thick, ductile, or directional burrs may fold rather than detach and may need brushing, cutting, or local removal first

· Cleaning and drying are part of the result: Residual abrasive, compound, or moisture can interfere with the next operation; ferrous parts may also need prompt drying and temporary corrosion protection

## H2：Frequently Asked Questions

### Tumbling deburring 能去除哪些毛刺？

它更适合较轻、较薄且位于介质可接触区域的 machining、stamping、sawing 或 casting 毛刺。厚毛刺、卷边、深孔内定向毛刺或牢固浇口残留可能需要先进行局部机械去除。

### Barrel tumbling 与 vibratory deburring 有什么区别？

两者都属于 mass finishing。Barrel tumbling 依靠滚筒旋转使料堆翻滚，通常产生较渐进的处理；vibratory finishing 通过振动使介质和零件产生相对运动，过程通常更便于观察与调整。应根据零件尺寸、强度、几何和目标 finish 选择，而不是默认某一种更好。

### Tumbling 会改变零件尺寸或公差吗？

会。该工艺从外露边缘和表面去除材料，孔口、薄边和突出特征可能比遮挡区域变化更快。关键配合、密封面、螺纹和明确的 edge radius 应在图纸中标注并经过样件验证。

### 精密件或外观件会在滚磨中碰伤吗？

有可能。批量处理中的 part-on-part contact 可能留下 nicks、擦痕或叠压变形。可通过控制装载量、调整介质、分隔处理或改用夹持式 finish 降低风险，但具体方案需按零件评估。

### Tumbling media 能进入孔、槽和内腔吗？

部分开放特征可以被接触，但深盲孔、窄槽、交叉孔和遮挡面不能假定获得与外表面相同的处理。还要防止介质卡在孔或螺纹中，因此 RFQ 应标注最小通道和不可残留区域。

### Tumbling deburring 可以得到指定 Ra 吗？

它可以降低部分表面峰值并改变整体 texture，但没有一个固定 Ra 可适用于所有材料和几何。若粗糙度是功能要求，应提供测量位置、方向和目标值，并通过试样确认 process window。

### Tumbling 可以把零件抛到镜面吗？

特定材料和多阶段 burnishing 或 polishing 路线可以提高亮度，但普通去毛刺 cycle 不等于镜面抛光。深刀纹、凹坑和前序缺陷也不会仅靠延长时间自动消失。

## H2: Frequently Asked Questions

### What burrs can tumbling deburring remove?

It is best suited to relatively light, thin burrs in areas that media can reach, including burrs from machining, stamping, sawing, or casting. Thick burrs, folded edges, directional burrs deep in a hole, or firmly attached gate remnants may need local mechanical removal first.

### What is the difference between barrel tumbling and vibratory deburring?

Both are mass-finishing methods. Barrel tumbling rotates the work mass so parts and media cascade, usually creating a gradual cutting action. Vibratory finishing uses vibration to generate relative motion and is generally easier to observe and adjust during the cycle. Selection should follow part size, strength, geometry, and finish target rather than an assumption that one method is always better.

### Will tumbling change part dimensions or tolerances?

Yes. The process removes material from exposed edges and surfaces. Hole entrances, thin edges, and protruding features may change faster than shielded areas. Critical fits, sealing faces, threads, and specified edge radii should be shown on the drawing and validated with samples.

### Can precision or cosmetic parts be nicked during tumbling?

Yes. Part-on-part contact in a batch can leave nicks, scuffs, or distortion from overlap. Load control, media changes, compartmental processing, or a fixtured finishing method may reduce the risk, but the approach must be reviewed for the actual part.

### Can tumbling media reach holes, slots, and internal cavities?

Some open features can be worked, but deep blind holes, narrow slots, cross-holes, and shielded surfaces should not be expected to match the outside surface. Media lodging in holes or threads is another risk, so the RFQ should identify the smallest passages and no-residue zones.

### Can tumbling deburring achieve a specified Ra?

It can lower some surface peaks and change the overall texture, but no single Ra applies to every material and geometry. If roughness is functional, provide the measurement location, direction, and target, then confirm the process window on samples.

### Can tumbling produce a mirror finish?

Selected materials may become brighter through a multi-stage burnishing or polishing route, but a normal deburring cycle is not mirror polishing. Deep tool marks, pits, and upstream defects will not automatically disappear by extending the cycle.

### How can I get a quote for tumbling deburring?

Upload the 3D CAD model, 2D drawing, material grade, quantity, part size, burr locations and photos, allowable edge break or radius, critical dimensions, cosmetic faces, cleanliness needs, and downstream finish. If contact marks or surface consistency are sensitive, include both an untreated part and a target sample when possible.
