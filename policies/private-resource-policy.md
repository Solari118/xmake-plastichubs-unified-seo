# Private Resource Policy

## Local Use

- The folders registered in `config/private-resource-sources.yaml` are approved knowledge and media sources for this project and `XMAKE-PlasticHubs-SEO-Blueprint`.
- Local project copies are allowed. Use `scripts/sync_private_resources.ps1`; never delete or rename source files.
- Load only task-relevant files. Keep confidential filenames, source paths, supplier identities, management material, and private notes out of public output.

## Image Transformation

- Treat APEX and 3D TIGER images as company-owned source material, not as public target-brand assets.
- Before XMAKE or PlasticHubs publication, replace every visible `APEX`, `TIGER`, or related logo/wordmark with the selected public brand's approved logo or wordmark. Check signage, clothing, equipment, packaging, screens, labels, reflections, and watermarks.
- Do not mix XMAKE and PlasticHubs in one public image.
- When no source brand is visible, still make a material visual edit before use. Valid edits include crop/perspective, composition, background, lighting/color, object arrangement, callout design, or privacy cleanup. Metadata-only changes, recompression, or filename changes do not qualify.
- For engineering drawings, preserve dimensions, geometry, tolerances, standards, and process meaning. Limit changes to layout, crop, title-block/privacy cleanup, annotation style, or non-technical presentation unless a technical revision is separately verified.
- The final asset must not be pixel-identical to the source. Record source file, target brand, modifications, logo asset, and residue check in the private asset ledger.

## Enterprise Knowledge

- Engineering data and process material may be used directly as private evidence and article/blog inputs.
- Business and management material is a conditional backup source.
- Public content may use approved facts and data but must not reveal the confidential library name, file path, internal author, management discussion, or unrelated private material.
- Conflicts, stale data, or regulated/exact claims require verification; do not silently prefer a private file over newer authoritative evidence.

## Packaging Gate

- This gate applies only when the user requests packaging, export, handoff, or deployment of either project, its Skills, Agents, Hooks, or resource bundle.
- Every packaging request requires a fresh question: whether to include `企业知识（保密）`.
- Do not reuse consent from an earlier package. If the user does not explicitly answer yes for that package, exclude enterprise knowledge.
- Local copies are not prohibited by this gate.
