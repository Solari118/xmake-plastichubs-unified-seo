# Evidence And Publication Policy

- Public artifacts use one selected brand.
- Public links default to the selected brand's domain.
- Competitor URLs, external source links, supplier identities, RapidDirect references, and private claim ledgers remain internal.
- Exact figures, standards, certifications, guarantees, regulated work, and safety-critical claims require applicable evidence.
- Live research status must be truthful: `Validated`, `Best-effort`, `Degraded`, `Unavailable`, or `Blocked` as the relevant skill defines.
- A public claim that cannot stand without disallowed attribution must be removed, rewritten from approved brand evidence, or marked `Needs approval`.
