## 2. Industry Knowledge Map

PlasticHubs should not compete as a generic all-material manufacturing marketplace. The strongest SEO direction is **plastic-only custom manufacturing**, led by **plastic CNC machining** and supported by material expertise, prototyping, low-volume production, process selection, engineering support and quality trust.

The industry map runs from CAD/RFQ and material selection, through CNC/3D printing/vacuum casting/injection molding, into inspection, delivery and downstream applications.

## 11. Execution Notes

- Build the CNC and quote/supplier pages first, not generic blog content.
- Keep public-facing copy focused on PlasticHubs’ own positioning and verified public claims.
- Do not reveal hidden corporate relationships in public SEO copy.
- Use competitor/reference pages only for benchmarking page structure, not for unsupported claims.
- Do not invent search volume, CPC, keyword difficulty, ranking position or traffic estimates.
- Mark exact SERP validation as needed before final URL/title copywriting.
- Certifications, factory, quality and equipment claims should be verified internally before adding certificate numbers, logo files or downloadable PDFs.

## 12. Assumptions And Validation Needs

- Exact SERP intent for high-conversion supplier/quote keywords remains to be manually checked.
- The final balance between US and UK spelling should be chosen based on target region page strategy. For EU, include injection moulding variants where relevant; for US, use injection molding.
- Plastic 3D printing and injection molding are important, but they should not outrank plastic CNC machining in the first build unless business priority changes.
- Case studies are strongly recommended once real projects can be shown.
