# Writer Quality Checklist

## Hard Fail

- Plastichubs/xmake appears in public copy or publishing fields.
- A third-party brand name, third-party URL, competitor link, `Source:` label, or external-research attribution appears in public copy without explicit approval.
- More than one public brand is mixed without an explicitly requested comparison.
- PlasticHubs is assigned an unsupported non-plastic service.
- A citation, standard, metric, tolerance, certification, guarantee, or competitor fact is fabricated.
- Commercial intensity conflicts with `COMMERCIAL_BRIDGE_MODE`.
- Placeholders or TODO text remain.
- `SERP_RESEARCH_REQUIRED=Yes` but the benchmark is missing or `Blocked` and the output is labeled final.
- The article copies or closely paraphrases competitor prose, distinctive headings, proprietary examples, tables, or one competitor's full outline.
- Ranking hypotheses are repeated as proven causes.
- An explicit user minimum length, readability target, or requested deliverable format is ignored in a final-labeled artifact.
- A local or sectional revision request is expanded into a full rewrite without necessity, or preferred source elements are removed without explanation.

## Quality Checks

- The article completes the audience's stated task and satisfies primary intent.
- Every `INTENT_COMPLETION_CRITERIA` item is satisfied, and the core answer appears in the opening or first core H2.
- The opening fits the problem and does not reuse generic setup language.
- Structure follows the topic rather than a fixed template.
- Each H2 has a distinct reader job; selection tables and “when to choose” sections do not repeat the same logic.
- Headings are specific and do not rely on generic `Quick Answer` or `Fast Guide` labels.
- The article adapts useful patterns across the five benchmark pages without becoming derivative.
- Documented SERP gaps and `DIFFERENTIATION_REQUIREMENTS` are visibly addressed.
- The final article adds coherent original value beyond a stitched summary of competitors.
- Keyword use is natural; variants and entities improve meaning.
- Technical claims include necessary conditions and limitations.
- Information gain is concrete and source-aware.
- Broker comparison is proportional and professional.
- FAQ, tables, lists, tools, and diagrams appear only when useful.
- Metadata, same-brand internal links, authorship, dates, image, and schema fields are addressed; external evidence remains private.
- Chinese precedes English and both versions remain aligned.
- English reads naturally at the supplied readability target, when one exists; Chinese and English use native punctuation and are aligned by meaning rather than word order.
- The opening uses a concrete SPEAR consequence and does not begin with a generic definition.
- A comparison article keeps both options visible under shared decision criteria; readers do not need to reconstruct the comparison from separate mini-guides.
- Public paragraphs contain no more than three sentences.
- DFM is tied to a concrete project risk rather than used as a generic service label.
- FAQs answer unresolved questions and do not duplicate the article body.
- No forbidden Anti-AI term appears.
- Legacy prompt metrics are absent unless target-brand evidence verifies the exact claim and scope.
- Requested preview or Word artifacts were created only at the correct stage, corrections were persisted, and visual QA claims match actual render evidence.
- Revision breadth matches `REVISION_SCOPE`, and `SOURCE_ELEMENTS_TO_PRESERVE` remain intact unless a documented defect requires change.
