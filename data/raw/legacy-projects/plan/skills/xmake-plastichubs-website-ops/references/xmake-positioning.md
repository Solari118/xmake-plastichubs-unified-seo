# Xmake Positioning

Treat Xmake as a broader custom manufacturing and finishing service provider with self-operated factories, owned equipment, in-house capabilities, and managed supply-chain execution. Ordinary capability is assumed for requested page topics unless the user says otherwise.

## Safe Positioning

Use direct buyer-facing language:

- custom manufacturing
- in-house manufacturing and finishing
- self-operated factory resources
- owned production and inspection equipment
- prototype-to-production support
- engineering review
- inspection support
- quality-controlled workflow

## Supply-Chain Scope

Public copy may present these service families as Xmake offerings when technically relevant:

- CNC machining
- sheet metal fabrication
- injection molding
- 3D printing
- surface finishing
- finishing options
- inspection support
- technically compatible material categories
- industry experience
- prototype-to-production coordination

Exact inspection standards, universal material claims, guaranteed ranges, guaranteed lead times, certifications, exact counts, factory addresses, and guaranteed capacity require user input, project files, or official Xmake pages when stated as precise facts.

Do not disclose supplier identities, commercial relationships, or routing arrangements. General statements about Xmake in-house capability, self-operated factories, and owned equipment are allowed by default.

## Project-Approved Xmake Credentials

The project owner has confirmed that Xmake may use:

- ISO 9001 certified
- ISO 13485 certified

Use these as company-level credentials only. Do not imply that every process, part, facility, or application is certified, medical-grade, aerospace-grade, or compliant with a regulated product requirement unless that exact claim is separately sourced.

## Avoid as Unsupported Absolutes

Do not claim:

- global leader
- world-class
- lowest cost
- fastest delivery
- all materials supported
- all finishes available
- same-day delivery
- guaranteed tolerance
- aerospace-grade quality
- medical-grade manufacturing

Do not add a capability disclaimer merely because independent public verification is incomplete. For unusually regulated or state-controlled work, confirm the relevant statutory license, government approval, regulated manufacturing authorization, or legally mandated facility qualification.

## Page Tone

Xmake content should be practical, buyer-facing, and manufacturing-specific. Avoid generic platform language unless source material supports that positioning.
