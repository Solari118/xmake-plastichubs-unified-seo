# Plastichubs Positioning

Plastichubs should be positioned around plastic CNC machining first.

## Core Position

Use Plastichubs for:

- plastic CNC machining
- custom plastic parts
- plastic prototypes
- low-volume production
- production support
- plastic CNC milling
- plastic CNC turning

## Main Services

Primary:

- Plastic CNC Machining
- CNC Milling
- CNC Turning

Secondary:

- Injection Molding
- Silicone Overmolding
- Plastic 3D Printing
- Vacuum Casting

Secondary services should support the plastic-parts buyer journey. They should not make Plastichubs sound like a broad manufacturing marketplace.

## Technical Boundary

When writing Plastichubs content:

- emphasize plastic substrates
- distinguish plastic machining from metal machining
- mention material, geometry, and tolerance dependence
- avoid claiming every plastic can be machined or finished the same way
- avoid metal-first process framing unless the page specifically compares plastic and metal considerations

## Unsafe Positioning

Do not describe Plastichubs primarily as:

- generic manufacturing
- broad supply chain
- metal machining
- marketplace
- all-process production platform
