# Surface Finishing Knowledge Base

Use this file as a boundary guide, not as a substitute for source documents. Do not generate process-specific pages without source inputs.

## Water Transfer Printing

Decorative film transfer for complex 3D surfaces. Can apply to selected plastic and metal substrates depending on base coat, activation, geometry, and topcoat.

Avoid claiming universal material compatibility.

## Electrophoresis / E-Coating

Mainly a metal corrosion-protection and uniform coating process. Do not present it as a plastic process unless verified source material explains a supported plastic-specific workflow.

## Engraving

Mechanical or CNC-based marking for logos, serial numbers, recessed text, tactile marks, and identification features. It may overlap with marking, but it is not the same as printing.

## Tumbling

Used for deburring, edge smoothing, batch finishing, and surface smoothing. Do not present it primarily as a decorative coating.

## Vacuum Plating / PVD

Can create decorative metallic finishes or wear-resistant thin films depending on the process. Do not imply all plastics are suitable. Adhesion, pretreatment, temperature, and substrate compatibility matter.

## Flocking

Creates a velvet-like texture for grip, decorative feel, or noise reduction. Substrate and adhesive compatibility matter.

## Enameling

Often metal, steel, or cast-iron focused. Use caution for plastic pages unless a source verifies the process and substrate compatibility.

## Silk Screen Printing

Used for printed graphics, logos, labels, and panels on flat or slightly curved surfaces. Applies to selected metals and plastics depending on ink and surface preparation.

## Required Caveat

For every finishing page, include material, geometry, surface preparation, and finish requirement caveats when source evidence is incomplete.
