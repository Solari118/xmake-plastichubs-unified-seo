# SEO Keyword And Query Rules

## Required Publishing Fields

- Suggested URL or existing URL
- Canonical URL status
- Meta Title
- Meta Description
- H1
- Primary Keyword
- Secondary Keywords
- Query Variants
- Key Entities
- Primary and secondary intent
- Suggested Internal Links
- External evidence links when required

## Natural Coverage

- Treat the primary keyword as the page's central topic, not a repetition target.
- Use it in the title or H1 when it accurately and naturally describes the page.
- Use natural variants, expert terminology, buyer modifiers, entities, and questions throughout the content where they help comprehension.
- Do not require the exact primary keyword in the first paragraph, an H2, FAQ, and body.
- Do not force exact-match repetition or density targets.
- Do not insert secondary keywords when they change meaning, scope, or brand positioning.

## Search Intent

Classify a dominant intent and, when useful, a secondary intent:

- transactional
- commercial investigation
- informational
- comparison
- troubleshooting/support
- standards/specification

## Internal And External Links

- Recommend internal links that advance the reader's task and reinforce the topic cluster.
- Use descriptive anchor text; do not use the same anchor mechanically.
- Link external official or primary sources for standards, technical claims, statistics, and definitions when publication needs independent evidence.
- Do not link PlasticHubs as a broad metal-manufacturing provider.

## GEO Support

- Keep important answers available as clear text.
- Use concise answer units with conditions and limitations.
- Clarify brands, services, processes, materials, and standards as distinct entities.
- Make structured data match visible content.
- Do not invent special AI markup or promise inclusion in generative answers.
