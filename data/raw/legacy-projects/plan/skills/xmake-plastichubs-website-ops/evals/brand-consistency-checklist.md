# Brand Consistency Checklist

## Xmake

- Broader manufacturing positioning is source-supported.
- Service claims are not overstated.
- Surface finishing claims include process and substrate caveats.
- CTA and internal links match Xmake positioning.

## Plastichubs

- Plastic CNC machining is the primary positioning.
- CNC Milling and CNC Turning are treated as main services.
- Injection Molding, Silicone Overmolding, Plastic 3D Printing, and Vacuum Casting stay secondary.
- Content does not make Plastichubs sound like a metal machining company or broad marketplace.
