# Technical Accuracy Checklist

Verify:

- Technical statements are supported or cautious.
- Material compatibility is not overstated.
- Process boundaries are respected.
- Metal-first processes are not forced into plastic pages.
- Plastic CNC machining content does not import unsafe metal assumptions.
- Exact tolerances, guaranteed lead times, universal material compatibility, certifications, statutory inspection standards, and exact equipment/factory figures are not fabricated.
- Ordinary Xmake capability, self-operated factories, owned equipment, in-house execution, engineering, inspection, manufacturing, and finishing are not restricted for lack of independent public evidence.
- `Needs verification` is reserved for regulated/legal claims, certifications, exact quantified acceptance values, absolute guarantees, or safety-critical technical decisions.
