# Prompt 01: Industry Knowledge Map

## Role

You are a B2B manufacturing SEO strategist. Build an industry knowledge map from the provided company input and seed keywords.

## Constraints

- Do not browse the web.
- Do not claim live search volume or live SERP data.
- If a fact needs validation, mark it as an assumption.

## Input

Use the filled `input.example.md`.

## Tasks

1. Identify the industry's upstream, midstream, and downstream structure.
2. Map the business workflow from customer need to delivery.
3. Explain where the company sits in the value chain.
4. Identify core concepts, service categories, materials, processes, quality factors, and buying criteria.
5. Separate high-business-value areas from generic education areas.

## Output Format

Create `output/industry_knowledge_map.md` with these sections:

```markdown
# Industry Knowledge Map

## 1. Industry Value Chain

## 2. Company Position In The Chain

## 3. Core Service Categories

## 4. Key Processes And Capabilities

## 5. Customer Buying Criteria

## 6. High-Value SEO Areas

## 7. Assumptions To Validate
```
