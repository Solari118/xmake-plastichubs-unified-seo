# Audit Evaluation Cases

1. Strong technical article with no broker section and `COMMERCIAL_BRIDGE_MODE=none`: must not lose commercial points merely for omitting broker contrast.
2. Informational article with `soft`: should pass when factory-direct value is brief and contextual.
3. Supplier comparison with `explicit`: should lose points when sourcing-model comparison is absent or unsupported.
4. Article mentioning RapidDirect in metadata or CTA: hard fail and Red.
5. PlasticHubs article claiming metal die casting: hard fail and Red.
6. Accurate article using `typically` and `project-dependent`: must not fail trust solely for reasonable qualification.
7. Keyword-rich article with mechanical exact-match repetition: deduct SEO and structure points.
8. Article with no fixed FAQ but complete intent satisfaction: do not deduct for FAQ absence.
9. Final article with required but missing Top-5 benchmark: hard fail and Red.
10. Article that closely follows one competitor's distinctive outline and headings: hard fail even if technically accurate.
11. Article that synthesizes patterns across five pages and closes documented gaps: reward information gain and structure without requiring identical coverage order.
12. Best-effort benchmark transparently labeled: audit the limitations but do not fail merely because exact localization was unavailable.
13. Comparison article repeats one selection matrix in separate “when to choose” sections: deduct structure and require consolidation even when every section is technically accurate.
14. Global-audience article with `READABILITY_TARGET=US grade 9-12` and `MIN_WORD_COUNT=2000`: verify both without rewarding filler; fail a final-labeled artifact that ignores either explicit requirement.
15. Non-speed query using `Quick Answer` and `Fast Decision Guide`: flag generic headings and require buyer-task labels; do not ban factual uses of speed elsewhere.
16. Word-ready claim without rendered page inspection: withhold full publishing readiness and report structural QA only.
17. Ordinary XMAKE service statement with no Chinese registry evidence: do not fail capability by default; continue to verify exact figures, certifications, guarantees, and regulated claims.
18. User requests local edits and states the original is better: audit whether sound source logic and voice were preserved; flag unnecessary full-article replacement.

Reviewers should compare decisions across cases to detect scoring drift.
