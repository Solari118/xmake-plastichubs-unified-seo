# **COMPETITOR INTELLIGENCE**

## Competitive Position Summary

- XMAKE wins deals primarily because quotes are faster: 3 minutes (instant) vs 1-2 days for competitors.
- XMAKE can compete on price: injection molding quotes are approximately 1/3 of competitor pricing (based on Trustpilot reviews).
- Shipping is typically 3-7 days via DHL/FedEx air freight since order quantities are small (3-12 parts), not ocean freight.
- Customers usually evaluate 2-3 options when sourcing these services.
