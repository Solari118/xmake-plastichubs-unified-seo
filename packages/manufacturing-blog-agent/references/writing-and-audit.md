# 写作与独立审核 / Writing and independent audit

## 正文 / Draft

1. 用已批准 Plan 中的主问题和文章类型决定开头。开头尽早回答真实问题，同时保留后文所需的条件与限制。/ Let the approved primary question and article type determine the opening. Answer the real question early, with necessary conditions.
2. 每个 H2 只承担一个读者任务，首段兑现标题承诺；H3 承担明确不同的子问题。不同主题可有不同 H2 数量、段落长短和证据形式。/ Give each H2 one reader task and make its first paragraph fulfill the heading. Use H3 for real subquestions. Section count, paragraph length, and evidence form vary by topic.
3. 参考文只可借鉴可解释的功能：问题顺序、对比维度、证据形式、节奏。正文、标题、案例和转折必须原创。/ Adapt explainable reference functions such as question order, comparison dimensions, evidence form, and pacing. Write original prose, headings, examples, and transitions.
4. FAQ 解答正文尚未解决的具体问题。CTA 只执行本次独立批准的 `FULL`、`LIGHT` 或 `NONE`，目标链接必须属于发布者并经过核验。/ FAQ answers unresolved specific questions. Follow only the approved CTA mode; verify the destination belongs to the publishing site.
5. 技术断言写明条件、单位、材料或工艺范围。未经证明的精确数值、资质、节省比例、交期或性能结论不得进入文章。/ State conditions, units, material, and process scope for technical claims. Exclude unsupported exact figures, credentials, savings, lead times, and performance outcomes.
6. 英语稿自然、适合本次读者方向；避免空泛开头、堆砌关键词、套用统一模板及无证据的优越性措辞。/ Write natural English for the selected reader mode. Avoid generic openings, keyword stuffing, fixed outlines, and unsupported superiority claims.

## 作者自检 / Writer self-check

作者核对 Plan 依赖、标题与正文一致性、全部必要问题、来源与断言、近期同站文章的语义角色和节奏、FAQ、CTA、内链、拼写与可读性。问题修复后才交独立 Audit；自检不得写 `GREEN`。

The writer checks Plan dependencies, heading/body alignment, complete reader questions, claims and sources, semantic and rhythm similarity to recent same-site articles, FAQ, CTA, internal links, language, and readability. Fix issues before independent Audit; writer self-check never issues `GREEN`.

## 独立 Audit / Independent Audit

独立审查者读取当前正文及其绑定的 Plan 和参考记录，按 `templates/audit-report.md` 记录事实、意图、结构适配、原创性、段落语义、FAQ、CTA、链接与语言；文章质量为 0–20 分，读者理解另记 0–4 分，不能合并分母。硬性缺口标记 `REVISION_REQUIRED` 或 `BLOCKED`。修订产生新版本和新哈希，重新审核后才能批准。

An independent reviewer reads the exact article, bound Plan, and reference record. Record factual, intent, adaptation, originality, section alignment, FAQ, CTA, link, and language findings in the audit template. Score article quality 0–20 and reader comprehension separately 0–4. Hard gaps require `REVISION_REQUIRED` or `BLOCKED`. A revision creates a new version and hash and requires re-audit before approval.
