## Quality Control Process

1. DFM Review — Front-end risk review for manufacturability and assembly reliability
2. IQC Inspection — Component and material verification before production release
3. SPI Inspection — Solder paste, placement, polarity, and assembly defect detection
4. X-Ray Inspection — BGA, QFN, hidden joints, and internal solder defect inspection
5. Flying Probe / E-Test — Open, short, and continuity checks for PCB and PCBA
6. Functional Test — Customized test setup based on product function and requirements
7. Final Shipment Inspection
