## 2. Company Position In The Chain

PlasticHubs should be positioned as a **plastic-focused custom parts manufacturing partner** rather than a broad “all-material online manufacturing marketplace.”

Recommended public positioning:

> PlasticHubs provides custom plastic CNC machining and plastic part manufacturing support for prototypes, low-volume production and production-ready custom plastic components.

Best-fit strategic role:

- Core: plastic CNC machining supplier.
- Commercial conversion center: custom CNC plastic parts quote / supplier page.
- Technical authority center: CNC plastic materials and plastic machining DFM.
- Expansion center: 3D printing, vacuum casting, injection molding and overmolding.
- Trust center: quality control, certifications, inspection and engineering support.

## 4. Key Processes And Capabilities

- Plastic CNC milling: complex profiles, pockets, slots, housings, fixtures and flat/3D geometries.
- Plastic CNC turning: bushings, spacers, rollers, sleeves, threaded or cylindrical plastic components.
- 5-axis plastic CNC machining: complex multi-side parts and high-precision geometries.
- Plastic 3D printing: rapid prototypes, complex forms and low-tooling early-stage validation.
- Vacuum / urethane casting: production-like prototypes and low-volume molded parts without full injection tooling.
- Injection molding: repeatable plastic parts when tooling and higher volumes are justified.
- Surface finishing: optical, cosmetic, functional and assembly-oriented finishing.
- Engineering support: DFM, material selection, tolerance review, RFQ preparation and process selection.
- Quality support: inspection, documented workflow, certification and traceability claims where publicly verified.
