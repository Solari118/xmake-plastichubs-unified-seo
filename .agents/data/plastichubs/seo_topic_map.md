# SEO Topic Map — PlasticHubs

## 1. Strategic Direction

The topic map uses **plastic CNC machining** as the commercial center. It then builds supporting clusters around custom CNC plastic parts, supplier/quote intent, CNC plastic materials, rapid prototyping, low-volume production, secondary plastic processes, industry applications, quality/DFM and buyer decision guides.

The goal is not to rank for every broad manufacturing term. The goal is to own a sharper category:

> custom plastic CNC machining and plastic part manufacturing for prototypes, low-volume production and production-ready custom components.
