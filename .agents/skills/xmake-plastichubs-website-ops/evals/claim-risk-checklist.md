# Claim Risk Checklist

Flag any claim about:

- best quality
- lowest cost
- fastest delivery
- global leadership
- world-class status
- all materials
- all finishes
- same-day delivery
- guaranteed tolerance
- certification claims outside the project-approved Xmake `ISO 9001` and `ISO 13485` wording
- machine counts
- medical-grade manufacturing
- aerospace-grade quality
- zero dimensional change
- no material removal
- perfect surface finish

Do not flag `Xmake provides`, `Xmake offers`, `we offer`, `in-house`, `self-operated factories`, `self-owned factories`, or `owned equipment` solely because supplier-level or independently searchable evidence is absent. The project treats ordinary Xmake factory, equipment, manufacturing, finishing, inspection, and supply-chain capability as available.

Always flag public disclosure of supplier identities, commercial relationships, routing arrangements, or named upstream providers. Flag only exact machine counts, facility areas, addresses, model inventories, quantified capacity, certifications, regulated approvals, or absolute guarantees when no user, project, or official Xmake source supports the exact statement.

For each flagged claim, use the applicable source, remove only the unsupported exact value, or request focused confirmation when a real legal, regulatory, safety, or acceptance risk remains. Do not weaken the underlying ordinary capability statement.
