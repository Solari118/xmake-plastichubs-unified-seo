# SERP Benchmark Review Rules

## Benchmark Completeness

Confirm that the brief records:

- search engine, locale, language, device, query, and date
- benchmark status: `Validated`, `Best-effort`, or `Blocked`
- SERP access status, user-browser verification status, and explicit downgrade approval evidence when status is `Best-effort`
- ten observed editorial/blog results or transparent exclusions/substitutions
- per-page title, URL, intent, primary audience, content format, H1/H2/H3 hierarchy, title alignment, opening answer point, narrative order, section proportions, table/list use and placement, pacing, style, comprehension load, English naturalness, AI-pattern risks, evidence form, commercial intensity, CTA, strengths, ranking hypotheses, and gaps
- calculated intent, audience, and format distributions plus their dominant classifications
- selected intent and format, angle-alignment status, specific user approval evidence for any deviation, and `PLAN_GATE_STATUS=PASS`
- cross-SERP common strengths, content gaps, borrowable patterns, patterns to avoid, differentiation requirements, and originality guardrails
- selected primary rhythm controller and bounded secondary structure-style references, article archetype, adaptation map, style profile, reference shape fingerprint, rhythm route, mandatory FAQ/CTA plans, and conditional module include/omit decisions
- site authority context, content history, indexation/canonical risk, keyword difficulty evidence, CTR context, competing-page strength, and verified internal-link deployment

Do not accept `Validated` when the source cannot establish the specified environment and organic positions.
Do not accept `Best-effort` when exact Google Top-10 was required unless the user explicitly approved that downgrade and the brief records the evidence. Unresolved Google verification or tool-permission blocks prevent GREEN.
Do not accept a title-only result list or let Audit infer approval for a planner-selected alternative.

## Draft Comparison

Evaluate whether the article:

- matches dominant intent without blindly copying the dominant format
- covers essential topics and entities from the benchmark
- addresses documented gaps
- delivers the required original information gain
- executes one selected primary controller plus only the bounded secondary functions declared in the brief
- executes the selected single- or multi-source structure/style contract
- remains coherent and independently useful
- differs meaningfully from recent same-brand articles unless the query justifies reuse
- passes the semantic/shape corpus gate even when exact H2 and n-gram overlap are zero
- keeps ranking explanations as hypotheses

## Originality Risks

Flag as a hard failure when there is close copying or lightly disguised paraphrase of competitor prose, distinctive headings, proprietary examples, unique tables, or a near-duplicate outline from one source. High-level structure and style adaptation is required; derivative expression is forbidden.

Shared generic topic headings are not automatically copying. Judge distinctiveness, sequence, expression, and concentration of overlap together.
