# Brief Quality Checklist

## Hard Fail

- A required handoff field is missing.
- Public variables mention RapidDirect.
- The selected brand is ambiguous or mixed.
- PlasticHubs is assigned a non-plastic service as its direct capability.
- A metric, standard, certification, tolerance, guarantee, search volume, or SERP fact is invented.
- `COMMERCIAL_HOOK` conflicts with `COMMERCIAL_BRIDGE_MODE`.
- `SERP_RESEARCH_REQUIRED=Yes` but fewer than ten analyzed editorial/blog results are supplied without a transparent exclusion/substitution record.
- Any Top-10 record lacks intent, primary audience, format, title alignment, H1/H2/H3 hierarchy, narrative sequence, opening answer point, section-length pattern, table/list placement, pacing, style, comprehension load, English naturalness, AI-pattern risk, evidence form, commercial intensity, CTA position, strengths, gaps, or ranking hypotheses.
- Declared intent, audience, or format distributions do not match the ten per-page classifications.
- The selected primary intent or governing format differs from the dominant route without explicit user approval evidence.
- `PLAN_GATE_STATUS` is not `PASS` at Plan-to-Info handoff.
- The brief claims exact Top-10 positions without the search environment or uses `Validated` when only best-effort search evidence exists.
- Any `SECTION_REFERENCE_MAP` item lacks its sequence index/reason or heading-scope mode.
- A core section follows FAQ without the exact affected headings, explicit exception, reader-benefit rationale, and supporting Top-10 URL in `FAQ_PLAN`.
- No primary structure reference is selected, or the selected references do not produce an executable adaptation map and style profile.
- `REFERENCE_SHAPE_FINGERPRINT` omits heading hierarchy, title alignment, opening answer point, narrative sequence, section proportions, table/list placement, comprehension load, English naturalness, or AI-pattern risk.
- `ARTICLE_RHYTHM_ROUTE` does not identify one primary controller or does not prevent sibling-article cadence convergence.
- `FAQ` or `CTA` is missing from `MANDATORY_MODULES`, `FAQ_PLAN` lacks 3–6 non-duplicative questions, or `CTA` lacks a topic-specific action, useful inputs, reader value, and verified direct same-brand URL.
- Site authority, content history/cannibalization, indexation/canonical risk, keyword difficulty, CTR context, competing-page strength, or internal-link deployment is omitted rather than marked and mitigated.
- Ranking hypotheses are presented as proven causes.
- Borrowable patterns authorize copying competitor language or a single competitor's outline.
- An explicit user constraint for readability, minimum length, preview, WordPress, or Word output is missing from the handoff.
- Revision scope or source-preservation requirements are dropped when the task starts from an existing draft.

## Quality Checks

- Persona, technical goal, intent, and decision stage are specific.
- Persona and intent conclusions are supported by the counted Top-10 distributions.
- `INTENT_COMPLETION_CRITERIA` are observable and identify the early core answer.
- `ANSWER_FIRST_FORMAT` fits the query instead of defaulting to a generic “Quick Answer.”
- `SECTION_ROLE_MAP` gives each H2 a distinct reader job and consolidates overlapping decision modules.
- Secondary keywords and query variants are useful, natural, and validation-labeled.
- Information gain is concrete rather than generic completeness language.
- GEO answer targets are sourceable and do not claim guaranteed visibility.
- The proposed architecture fits the topic instead of repeating a fixed template.
- The Top-10 synthesis identifies shared strengths, gaps, borrowable patterns, and explicit differentiation requirements.
- One or more selected references visibly control the archetype, structure, pacing, evidence form, and high-level style.
- FAQ and CTA are mandatory but their heading, form, placement, and wording are intentionally varied; tables, RFQs, DFM, failure modes, answer-first blocks, and conclusions are selected or omitted intentionally.
- The planned article adds value beyond the ten benchmark pages rather than producing a composite summary.
- Internal links have verified URLs, contextual anchors, and planned placements.
- Broker contrast intensity matches `none`, `soft`, or `explicit`.
- Evidence, internal links, CTA, and publishing fields are actionable.
- The brief is bilingual when project rules apply.
- User-set readability and artifact-delivery requirements are preserved without becoming universal defaults.
