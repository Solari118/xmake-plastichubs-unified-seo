# Brief Quality Checklist

## Hard Fail

- A required handoff field is missing.
- Public variables mention RapidDirect.
- The selected brand is ambiguous or mixed.
- PlasticHubs is assigned a non-plastic service as its direct capability.
- A metric, standard, certification, tolerance, guarantee, search volume, or SERP fact is invented.
- `COMMERCIAL_HOOK` conflicts with `COMMERCIAL_BRIDGE_MODE`.
- `SERP_RESEARCH_REQUIRED=Yes` but fewer than five observed or documented results are supplied without a transparent exclusion/substitution record.
- The brief claims exact top-five positions without the search environment or uses `Validated` when only best-effort search evidence exists.
- Ranking hypotheses are presented as proven causes.
- Borrowable patterns authorize copying competitor language or a single competitor's outline.
- An explicit user constraint for readability, minimum length, preview, WordPress, or Word output is missing from the handoff.
- Revision scope or source-preservation requirements are dropped when the task starts from an existing draft.

## Quality Checks

- Persona, technical goal, intent, and decision stage are specific.
- `INTENT_COMPLETION_CRITERIA` are observable and identify the early core answer.
- `ANSWER_FIRST_FORMAT` fits the query instead of defaulting to a generic “Quick Answer.”
- `SECTION_ROLE_MAP` gives each H2 a distinct reader job and consolidates overlapping decision modules.
- Secondary keywords and query variants are useful, natural, and validation-labeled.
- Information gain is concrete rather than generic completeness language.
- GEO answer targets are sourceable and do not claim guaranteed visibility.
- The proposed architecture fits the topic instead of repeating a fixed template.
- The Top-5 synthesis identifies shared strengths, gaps, borrowable patterns, and explicit differentiation requirements.
- The planned article adds value beyond the five benchmark pages rather than producing a composite summary.
- Broker contrast intensity matches `none`, `soft`, or `explicit`.
- Evidence, internal links, CTA, and publishing fields are actionable.
- The brief is bilingual when project rules apply.
- User-set readability and artifact-delivery requirements are preserved without becoming universal defaults.
