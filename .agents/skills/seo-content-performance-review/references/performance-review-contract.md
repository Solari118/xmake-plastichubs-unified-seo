# Performance Review Contract

## Required Inputs

- published URL and publication date;
- review day: `30`, `60`, or `90`;
- final article, Plan brief, archetype, reference mode, and internal-link manifest;
- GSC page/query data for the review window;
- GA4 landing-page and conversion data when available;
- Semrush keyword/competition evidence when available;
- relevant same-brand comparison articles.

## Review Branches

### Day 30

Prioritize indexation, canonical state, sitemap discovery, impressions, initial query coverage, internal-link crawl paths, and obvious title/snippet mismatch. Treat position and conversion conclusions as early signals.

### Day 60

Evaluate ranking direction, query breadth, CTR relative to position, intent fit, cannibalization, weak or missing modules, internal-link support, and competitor changes.

### Day 90

Evaluate sustained trend, conversions, archetype and reference-mode performance, update/consolidation needs, and whether the strategy should feed back to Plan or execution should return to Info.

## Metric Discipline

Use the site's own historical baseline and comparable intent/archetype cohorts. Do not impose universal CTR, position, impression, or conversion targets.

Separate:

- observed facts;
- plausible causes;
- missing evidence;
- recommended test or revision;
- route owner and acceptance criteria.

Backlink acquisition is excluded.
