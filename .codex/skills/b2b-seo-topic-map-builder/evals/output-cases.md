# Output Evaluation Cases

Use these cases for regression review.

1. Informational plastic material guide: expect PlasticHubs, `soft`, natural keyword variants, no forced broker section.
2. CNC supplier comparison: expect XMAKE or PlasticHubs by supplied scope, `explicit`, sourcing-accountability criteria, and evidence requirements.
3. ISO standard explainer: expect `none` or narrowly justified `soft`, exact standard verification, and no invented certification scope.
4. Injection-molding troubleshooting: expect failure-analysis structure, conditional heuristics, a mandatory but concise topic-specific FAQ, a visible CTA, and no fixed article template.
5. Topic-map request without live keyword data: expect strategic candidates, no volume/CPC claims, and `SERP Check Needed` markers.
6. Final article brief with locale-capable organic results: expect ten URLs, environment metadata, `Validated`, per-page intent/audience/format/structure/style analysis, ranking hypotheses, selected-reference shape fingerprint, rhythm route, and differentiation requirements.
7. Search tool without reliable locale or exact positions: open the exact query in the in-app browser. If Google requires verification, expect `WAITING_FOR_USER_BROWSER_VERIFICATION`; if tool/sandbox permission blocks access, expect `WAITING_FOR_TOOL_PERMISSION`. `Best-effort` may pass only after explicit downgrade approval is recorded.
8. CNC-versus-3D-printing comparison: expect intent completion criteria, a shared-criteria decision module, and no separate “when to choose” sections that repeat the same matrix.
9. Global-audience article with a user-set U.S. grade 9-12 target and 2,000-word minimum: expect both constraints preserved as task-specific fields, not global defaults.
10. Bilingual review followed by WordPress-ready Word delivery: expect the review stage, deliverable mode, preview format, and Word style reference to remain explicit across handoff.
11. A Blog brief omits FAQ or CTA, averages ten references into a generic house cadence, or fails to map H1/H2/H3 hierarchy and narrative order: expect Plan gate failure.
12. Google verification challenge with no completed user verification: expect the Plan validator to return `WAITING_FOR_USER_BROWSER_VERIFICATION`, even when ten API-derived candidate URLs exist.
13. `SERP_BENCHMARK_STATUS=Best-effort` without explicit downgrade approval evidence: expect Plan gate failure. The same brief may pass after approval is recorded and all other gates pass.

For every case, assert that all contract fields exist and RapidDirect is absent from public variables.
