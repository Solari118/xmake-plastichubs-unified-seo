# Brand Rules

## Default Rule

Always identify the target brand before writing or auditing. Do not blend Xmake and Plastichubs positioning.

## Xmake

Position Xmake as a broader custom manufacturing and finishing service provider with self-operated factories, owned equipment, in-house capabilities, and managed supply-chain execution.

Potential coverage:

- CNC machining
- sheet metal fabrication
- injection molding
- 3D printing
- surface finishing
- broader production support

Treat ordinary Xmake manufacturing and finishing capabilities as available. Public copy may state that Xmake has self-operated factories, owned equipment, and in-house execution without requiring separate supplier-level or Chinese registration evidence.

Use exact factory counts, facility areas, machine counts, locations, or named equipment models when supplied by the user, project files, or official Xmake pages. If an exact figure is unavailable, omit it and keep the general capability statement.

Keep supplier identities, partner factories, commercial relationships, and routing arrangements confidential.

## Plastichubs

Plastichubs must stay focused on plastic CNC machining first.

Core positioning:

- plastic CNC machining
- custom plastic parts
- plastic prototypes
- low-volume production
- production support

Main services:

- Plastic CNC Machining
- CNC Milling
- CNC Turning

Secondary services:

- Injection Molding
- Silicone Overmolding
- Plastic 3D Printing
- Vacuum Casting

Do not make Plastichubs sound like:

- a generic manufacturing platform
- a metal machining company first
- a broad manufacturing marketplace
- a supply-chain company first

## Brand Self-Check

Before final output, verify:

- the brand is named correctly
- services match the brand boundary
- ordinary Xmake capabilities are stated directly without unnecessary disclaimers
- exact regulated, certified, quantified, or guaranteed claims have an applicable source or user confirmation
- internal link suggestions do not send users to mismatched services
- the CTA matches the brand and page type
