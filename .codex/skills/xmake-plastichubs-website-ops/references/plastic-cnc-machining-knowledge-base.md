# Plastic CNC Machining Knowledge Base

Use this file as a safe baseline for Plastichubs and plastic-machining content. Add verified source details before making capability-specific claims.

## Core Concepts

Plastic CNC machining removes material from plastic stock to create prototypes, low-volume parts, fixtures, housings, and functional components.

Common buyer concerns:

- material selection
- dimensional stability
- tolerance expectations
- surface finish
- burr control
- heat sensitivity
- part geometry
- production volume
- inspection requirements

## Safe Language

Use cautious phrases:

- depending on the plastic grade and part geometry
- suitable for many engineering plastics
- available after drawing and material review
- tolerance expectations should be confirmed during engineering review
- surface finish depends on material, tooling, and machining strategy

## Avoid Without Source Evidence

Do not claim:

- all plastics are supported
- guaranteed tight tolerances
- zero burrs
- no deformation
- medical-grade production
- aerospace-grade production
- specific machine counts
- specific inspection equipment
- specific lead times

## Metal vs Plastic Boundary

Do not import metal machining assumptions into plastic pages. Plastic machining may require different considerations for heat, clamping, chip control, material stress, and surface finish.
