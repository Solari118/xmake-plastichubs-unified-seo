# Rights and synthesis rules

## Default

Public WeChat articles remain third-party copyrighted material. Public accessibility does not grant republication rights. Default every captured source to `research-only`.

## Allowed project use

- identify topics, terminology, questions, process mechanisms, and evidence gaps;
- preserve a private source record and normalized text for analysis;
- quote only a short necessary excerpt in the private ledger;
- independently verify factual leads;
- synthesize multiple sources into a new article architecture and original explanation;
- record disagreement or uncertainty.

## Prohibited public use

- full or near-full reproduction;
- close translation used as replacement prose;
- paragraph-by-paragraph synonym substitution;
- copied distinctive headings, tables, cases, diagrams, photographs, or illustrations;
- omission of attribution where a public quotation is explicitly approved and used;
- using the source solely to avoid duplicate-content detection.

## Permission states

- `research-only`: default; no public copying.
- `owned`: user confirms they own the source and necessary rights.
- `permission-confirmed`: user supplies or confirms publication permission and its scope.

Even with permission, technical claims must pass the project evidence gate and brand policy.
