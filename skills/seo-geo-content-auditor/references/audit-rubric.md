# SEO/GEO Article Audit Rubric

Score each dimension independently. Hard gates override the total.

| Dimension | Points | Standard |
|---|---:|---|
| Intent And Task Completion | 3 | Uses the approved opening mode, satisfies the explicit intent-completion criteria, matches dominant SERP intent, and resolves the primary reader job at the point required by the selected reference contract. |
| Technical Depth And Information Gain | 4 | Provides accurate conditions, limitations, decision rules, failure modes, examples, or evidence beyond generic summaries and visibly closes benchmark gaps. |
| SEO And Query Coverage | 2 | Clear topic, useful title/H1, natural primary and secondary coverage, essential benchmark entities/questions, and relevant internal links without stuffing. |
| GEO And Citation Readiness | 2 | Clear answer units, sourceable claims, entity clarity, useful subquestions, and traceable private evidence without unnecessary public third-party attribution or outbound links. |
| Brand And Commercial Fit | 3 | Correct single brand, correct service scope, and commercial intensity matching `none`, `soft`, or `explicit`. |
| Trust And Evidence | 3 | Claims, standards, metrics, certifications, comparisons, and qualifiers are accurate and appropriately supported. |
| Structure, Reference Adaptation, Corpus Diversity, And Tone | 2 | The article executes the approved H1/H2/H3 hierarchy, title alignment, answer timing, narrative sequence, section proportions, table/list placement, rhythm route, archetype, and style profile; differs meaningfully from the recent same-brand corpus; reads naturally; avoids derivative copying, unnecessary complexity, and mechanical AI cadence. |
| Publishing Readiness | 1 | Metadata, canonical status, authorship, dates, images, verified same-brand internal links, mandatory visible FAQ, mandatory visible topic-specific CTA, and schema recommendation are addressed; prohibited public third-party links are absent; explicit preview/Word delivery requirements are truthfully verified. |

## Deduction Rules

- Do not deduct merely because the article lacks a table, exact keyword position, fixed word count, or paragraphs of exactly three sentences. FAQ and CTA are mandatory Blog gates, but their placement and form must vary with intent and reference evidence.
- Deduct when structure impairs comprehension or when a useful artifact is missing for the actual decision.
- Apply a word-count or readability requirement only when the user or brief explicitly supplied it. Do not reward padding used to reach a minimum.
- Deduct when the same decision logic appears in multiple modules or a generic heading hides the section's actual reader job.
- A draft that ignores its selected reference structure/style or unjustifiably repeats the recent corpus cannot score GREEN.
- Zero identical H2s or zero n-gram overlap does not prove diversity. Repeated semantic roles, logic order, H2 count, section proportions, paragraph cadence, table/list density, FAQ/CTA form, or ending pattern fail the corpus gate when unjustified.
- Deduct for unsupported certainty; do not deduct for technically necessary qualifiers.
- A missing publishing field can be `Not provided` without hard failure when the article is still in review stage, but it prevents full publishing-readiness points.
