# SERP Benchmark Review Rules

## Benchmark Completeness

Confirm that the brief records:

- search engine, locale, language, device, query, and date
- benchmark status: `Validated`, `Best-effort`, or `Blocked`
- five observed results or transparent exclusions/substitutions
- per-page title, URL, intent, outline, strengths, ranking hypotheses, and gaps
- cross-SERP common strengths, content gaps, borrowable patterns, patterns to avoid, differentiation requirements, and originality guardrails

Do not accept `Validated` when the source cannot establish the specified environment and organic positions.

## Draft Comparison

Evaluate whether the article:

- matches dominant intent without blindly copying the dominant format
- covers essential topics and entities from the benchmark
- addresses documented gaps
- delivers the required original information gain
- uses useful patterns from multiple pages rather than imitating one page
- remains coherent and independently useful
- keeps ranking explanations as hypotheses

## Originality Risks

Flag as a hard failure when there is close copying or lightly disguised paraphrase of competitor prose, distinctive headings, proprietary examples, unique tables, or a near-duplicate outline from one source.

Shared generic topic headings are not automatically copying. Judge distinctiveness, sequence, expression, and concentration of overlap together.
