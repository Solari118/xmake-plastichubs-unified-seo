# XMAKE / PlasticHubs Company Assets

## Execution Boundary

This reference supplies brand and evidence rules, not a bank of automatically publishable metrics.

## Brand Scope

- Select one public brand per artifact.
- XMAKE may cover its supported manufacturing, finishing, engineering, inspection, and supply-chain scope.
- PlasticHubs remains focused on plastic manufacturing, including applicable plastic machining, molding, prototyping, and low-volume production routes.
- Do not present metal-only processes as direct PlasticHubs services.

## Evidence Priority

1. Explicit current user input and approved project files.
2. Current official target-brand pages.
3. Applicable primary technical sources and standards.
4. Internal shared-supply-chain evidence when it applies to the selected brand and remains private.

If sources conflict, record the conflict and avoid the claim until it is resolved.

## Public And Private Separation

- RapidDirect and supplier identities may appear only in private internal evidence when relevant. They are forbidden in public XMAKE or PlasticHubs copy, metadata, CTA, schema, and image text.
- Competitor names, URLs, and research notes stay in the private Plan benchmark or claim ledger.
- Public links default to the selected brand's domain.

## Ordinary Capability Defaults

Ordinary XMAKE manufacturing, finishing, self-operated factory, owned equipment, engineering, inspection, and supply-chain capability language is project-approved when relevant to the service scope. This does not approve exact counts, locations, dates, capacities, tolerances, delivery promises, guarantees, certifications, legal qualifications, or regulated work.

## Exact-Claim Gate

Historical prompt values—including facility counts, partner counts, user or shipment totals, machine counts, revenue, delivery percentages, lead times, tolerance tables, certification lists, and savings claims—are unverified until the current task supplies applicable evidence.

Do not copy exact values from legacy prompts or `data/raw/legacy-projects/`. Mark them `Needs verification` or omit them.

## Commercial Positioning

Use `COMMERCIAL_BRIDGE_MODE`:

- `none`: no sourcing-model contrast.
- `soft`: one contextual engineering, inspection, traceability, or accountability bridge.
- `explicit`: factual sourcing-model comparison when the query is commercial.

Do not use attack labels or invent markup, savings, speed, or superiority.

## Technical Use

Use DFM, inspection, traceability, material records, quotation inputs, and acceptance controls as concrete engineering mechanisms. Process limits remain geometry-, material-, finish-, and project-dependent unless exact evidence proves the stated boundary.
